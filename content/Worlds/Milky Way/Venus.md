# Overview  
**Venus** is a planet scorched by pressure and flame, its thick sulfuric atmosphere masking the silent, ancient evil that lurks beneath its molten crust. Though considered uninhabitable by conventional standards, it is anything but lifeless. The planet once held the **Infernal Gate**, an active portal used by the demonic forces of the **Obsidian Dominion** to exile the Necromorphs—twisted horrors born from God's failed virus.

In modern galactic history, Venus is remembered not for its natural state, but for its role as the site of the **Venusian Portal Incident** in the late 20th century. Now sealed by nuclear bombardment, the gate lies dormant, buried under layers of vitrified rock and irradiated ash. However, recent energy fluctuations have caused unease in the highest tiers of the **UCG** military command.

# History  
Venus became a focal point in the occult Cold War of the late 20th century. In 1990, during the delayed **Venera 7** mission, Soviet and American scientists uncovered an artificial anomaly beneath the dense clouds—a gateway unlike anything previously seen. This structure matched ancient celestial records, suggesting it was the very portal used in the **Great Exile**, when Saint Peter cast out both demons and Necromorphs.

The truth was devastating: the portal was active, and the Necromorphs had already passed through it long ago.

An immediate joint task force was formed by both superpowers, descending into the furnace of Venus to assess the threat. By the time they arrived, it was too late. The Necromorphs were gone, scattered across galaxies, and the demons’ prison had already failed.

Fearing the portal’s reactivation, the United States and the Soviet Union orchestrated **Operation Cerberus**—a last-ditch effort involving nuclear bombardment and dimensional freezing tech. The portal was sealed… temporarily. But Venus was forever marked.

Today, the planet is treated as a **Class Omega Containment Zone**. UCG starships are forbidden from approaching, and automated warning drones patrol its orbit. Still, rumors persist among starfarers and demonic cults that the **Infernal Gate** pulses once more.

# Cultural Significance  
To the **Obsidian Dominion**, Venus is both a cursed tomb and a sacred landmark. It marks their greatest failure—the loss of control over the Necromorphs—and also their origin, as the first planet to be touched by demonic presence in the modern era. Many believe that if the Gate is ever fully restored, the Dominion’s ancient powers will return with it.

For the **UCG**, Venus is a cautionary tale. A reminder that not all threats can be bombed into silence—and that some portals, once opened, are never truly closed.
