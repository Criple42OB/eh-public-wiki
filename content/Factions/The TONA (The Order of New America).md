# Overview  
The **Order of New America (TONA)** was a short-lived but terrifying superpower that emerged from the ashes of the United States during a time of global upheaval. Fueled by advanced military technology, nationalistic doctrine, and an obsessive pursuit of dominance, the TONA rapidly escalated from a world power to a planetary tyrant. Its brutal legacy is now remembered as one of conquest, nuclear devastation, and rebellion.

# Origins and Expansion (2002–2009)  
TONA’s roots trace back to the early 21st century, during a time of divine revelation and planetary instability. By 2002, humanity had confirmed the existence of both **Heaven** and **Hell**—triggering mass ideological shifts. The angels, foreseeing human aggression, evacuated Earth to the distant world of **Prometheus** in the **Exodus Galaxy**. Meanwhile, the United States seized upon the chaos, initiating a series of aggressive conquests.

In 2005, after surviving an assassination attempt, the U.S. launched a full-scale invasion of Canada. Within months, the nation had subjugated Central America and Mexico, deploying nuclear warheads against Mexico City, Monterrey, and Guadalajara. These strikes rendered vast regions radioactive and uninhabitable.

# Rebranding and Collapse (2016–2019)  
The U.S. officially rebranded as **The Order of New America** in 2016 following a political coup under a totalitarian regime. Its doctrine was centered on surveillance, expansionism, and technological supremacy. But internal fractures emerged.

In 2018, civil war erupted as the **Peacekeepers**, an insurgent faction born from within, launched a full-scale rebellion against TONA rule. Their resistance was soon bolstered by international powers such as the **New Russian Empire (NRE)** and the **Democratic Republic of the Congo (DRC)**, both declaring open war on TONA.

In an unprecedented event, both angelic and demonic factions—**The Galactic Faction of the Fallen Holy** and **The Obsidian Dominion**—intervened. Originally united in their goal of punishing humanity, they quickly turned on one another, with the angels siding with the Peacekeepers to avoid empowering the demonic warlords.

By 2019, the TONA’s infrastructure was collapsing. Battles were lost on all fronts. With its leadership fractured, the nation surrendered. From its ruins, the **United Coalition Against Tyranny (UCT)** was born.

# Military  
The TONA military combined cybernetic augmentation, AI-led command structures, and devastating weaponry. **Blacksteel Titans**—towering mech units powered by fusion cores—were deployed across occupied zones. Entire cities were monitored by AI surveillance grids known as **WatcherNet**, which enforced loyalty through biometric scanning and thought-suppression implants.

# Legacy  
Though the TONA ceased to exist by 2019, remnants of its regime remain buried across Earth and on lunar installations. Ruined databanks, AI cores, and dormant mech legions are occasionally unearthed by scavengers—or activated by accident. TONA's brutal efficiency and obsession with control remain cautionary tales in galactic history, remembered as a dark prelude to humanity’s interstellar age.
