# Overview
- Galactic monarchy and successor of the United Kingdom
- Engaged in a 300-year war against the dwarven planet Scrashandif
- Reigned by Ratlukhashaka the Eternal for centuries
- Holds control over several planetary colonies
- Maintains an archaic but technologically advanced military
- Honors etiquette, tradition, and martial discipline
- Officially neutral in celestial conflicts; maintains research and archival divisions on both demons and angels

## Notable Traits
- Reinvented imperialism through planetary conquest
- Practices cultural supremacy under the guise of "civilizing efforts"
- Soldiers retain ceremonial uniforms even in space operations
- Known for strict hierarchy, deeply entrenched nobility, and generational military service

## Relations with Others
- **Alfifa**: Distrusts their anti-hierarchy revolution but respects their military discipline.
- **UCG**: Maintains distance; resisted absorption. Now observes the UCG's decline with strategic patience.
- **Seraphic Concord and Obsidian Dominion:** Officially neutral. Secretly gathers extensive knowledge and prophecy archives on both.

## Military
- Known as the **Imperial Royal Forces**
- Combines traditional ranking systems with AI-enhanced battlefield management
- Notable for legendary discipline, honor-bound tactics, and elite orbital fleets
