# Overview
The Seraphic Concord is the divine union of angelic hosts tasked with the maintenance of celestial order and divine law. Governed by Saint Peter and the High Choir, the Concord balances grace with judgment, light with fire. Though once distant from mortal affairs, the rise of intergalactic conflict forced their intervention, culminating in their full-scale engagement during the First Galactic War.

# History
Formed in the earliest days of the cosmos, the Concord once focused solely on spiritual domains. However, the emergence of the Necromorph threat and mortal chaos led to the militarization of their forces. After centuries of warfare, the First Galactic War ended with a mutual peace treaty signed between the Concord and the Obsidian Dominion. Both sides agreed to cease hostilities and disarm major military formations. Despite this, tensions remain high and celestial rearmament has been rumored.

# Military
The Seraphic Concord maintains multiple ranks of angelic forces, from the common **Celestials** to the elite **Thrones**, **Dominions**, and **Virtues**. Their most powerful force, the **Empyrean Guard**, serves as the divine shock troopers of the High Choir. Their warships—known as **Cathedrals of Flame**—are capable of bending both physical and ethereal matter, fusing divine magic with ancient technology.
