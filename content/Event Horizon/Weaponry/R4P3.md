### R4P3-2058 Custom Spec
**Class:** Plasma Revolver
**Faction:** Unaffiliated (Manufactured by Exteria)
**Base Specs:** Short-range, Massive injury-inducing.

**Standard Configuration:**
- 5-charge Experimental Plasma Delivery Device
- Enlarged barrel, Grip, Hammer, and Cylinder yoke

**Notes:**
- The R4P3-2058 was originally developed by Exteria to be a collectors piece apart of their 30-year Heritage Collection. Modeled off of the Chiappa Rhino, Lex found this gun quite interesting, and requested the UCG manufactur one to his custom spec.
- The R4P3-2058 Custom Spec is capable of dealing massive damage to any combatant, though recoil is a concern if used in the wrong hands.
- It is rumoured that you may only wield the R4P3-2058 Custom Spec once you are considered worthy by the firearm.
