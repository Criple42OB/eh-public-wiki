### BFFA-2 Electron Railgun
**Class:** Electron Projecting Anti-Everything weapon 
**Faction:** UCG (Manufactured by Proximust)  
**Base Specs:** Long-range, Devastatingly explosive if damaged.

**Standard Configuration:**
- A large scope capable of up to 24x magnification 

**Notes:**
- The BFFA-2 Electron Railgun was manufactured by Proximust for the Proxima Federation's elite "Extinction Event" soldiers, The railgun is capable of leveling approximately ~640m (~2099ft) if attached to the users central-power reactor. However, Testing concludes that if used in this configuration the user will undergo a sleep-cycle by its energy depletion protection systems.
- Mary has modified a BFFA-2 Electron Railgun to be capable of connecting directly to the power of a Dyson Sphere. However, Only theoretically would this weapon be able to destroy an entire planet under these circumstances.
