### H.I.V.E 
**Class:** Special Ordnance Launcher
**Faction:** Unaffiliated (Manufactured by Exteria with Mary's cooperation)
**Base Specs:** Short-to-medium range, area denial, swarm deployment.

**Standard Configuration:**
- Six-round rotating canister magazine
- Fires hardened micro-drone pods on impact
- Each pod on detonation releases a swarm of proximity-triggered nano-drones that home in on nearby hostiles

**Notes:**
- The H.I.V.E was originally a UCG prototype shelved due to cost and production complexity. Mary reverse-engineered the canister deployment mechanism and refined the nano-drone targeting subroutines, making it viable for field use. Kat was the natural candidate to carry it given her scouting role.
- The nano-drone swarms do not distinguish between combatants and non-combatants at close range. Kat is briefed to deploy with caution in civilian-adjacent environments.
- Mary's involvement means the drone firmware receives intermittent wireless updates from her wrist computer mid-operation, occasionally improving tracking accuracy in real time.
- Due to its complexity, ammunition resupply in the field is nearly impossible without Mary's direct support.
