### High Awareness system
**Class:** Cybernetic spinal implant
**User:** Sydney "Hazard" Navarro
**Platform:** Users central nervous system
**Base Specs:** Awareness enhancement, base for Pridwen system, reaction enhancement.

**Standard configuration**

- Cybernetic implant directly installed onto user's spinal cord
- Tweaked to lowest setting as it begins settling into the users brain
- Enhanced threat assessment, capable of triggering Pridwen systems on it's own if power is not an issue.

**Limitations**

- The HALO system can easily overwhelm the user if they are not eased into the heightened senses, overstimulation is still very present even at low settings.
- Requires extensive mental training to fully understand the heightened senses.
