# Infantry Weapons - Event Horizon Roleplay

## Overview
The following document outlines the primary infantry weapons utilized by military forces across the Event Horizon universe. Most primary weapons are inspired by or derived from UNSC designs from Halo, while attachments and customization options follow Modern Warfare specifications. This allows for a diverse range of loadouts tailored to specific combat roles.

---

## Assault Rifles

### Exteria Assault Rifle
**Class:** Primary Assault Rifle  
**Faction:** Universal (UCG Primary Issue)  
**Base Specs:** Fully automatic, medium range, reliable

**Standard Configuration:**
- Iron sights or standard optics

**Popular Modifications:**
- ACOG sight (4x magnification, thermal capable)
- 3mw Red Laser (tactical aiming)
- Tactical Grip (improved control during sustained fire)
- Removable Suppressor (subsonic ammunition compatible)
- Tracer Rounds (standard or red trace)

**Notable Users:**
- Fletch "Cripple" Angel - Modified with ACOG, 3mw red laser, grip, and removable suppressor with tracer rounds
- Gull "Seagull" De Guzman - Exteria H6 variant with extended clip, red laser, and grip

**Notes:** The Exteria platform is the workhorse of UCG infantry. Its modular design allows for rapid reconfiguration, making it ideal for squad-based operations. The H6 variant has a slightly higher rate of fire and improved magazine capacity.

## Ammunition Types

### Standard Issue
- **Ball Ammunition:** Standard practice rounds, full metal jacket
- **FMJ (Full Metal Jacket):** Armor-piercing capability
- **Tracer Rounds:** Visible trajectory for range correction and team coordination (red, blue, green trace options)

### Specialized
- **Subsonic Rounds:** Reduced noise, designed for suppressor use (tracer or non-tracer)

### Armor-Piercing
- **Tracer Variants:** FMJ with tracer capability for long-range precision fire
- **Heavy Anti-Materiel:** For use in SRS 99-S5 class weapons

| Attachment | Function | Notes |
|-----------|----------|-------|

| 3mw Laser (Red) | Enhanced marking | Higher visibility, greater distance |
| Tactical Grip | Control | Improved recoil management |
| Vertical Foregrip | Stability | Hip-fire and sustained fire accuracy |
| Extended Magazine | Capacity | Increased ammunition before reload |

### Sound Suppressors
**Type:** Removable (quick-attach compatible)  
**Effect:** Reduces muzzle report and muzzle flash  
**Compatible With:** Subsonic ammunition for best effect  
**Trade-off:** Slight velocity reduction, increased sound signature is eliminated

## Notes on Customization Philosophy

The UCG military emphasizes **modular design** allowing rapid reconfiguration for specific missions. No two soldiers carry identical loadouts—personalization is encouraged based on:

1. **Combat Role** - Snipers carry long-range options; support specialists carry heavy suppressive fire
2. **Mission Profile** - Infiltration emphasizes suppressors; assault operations favor extended magazines and optics
3. **Squad Coordination** - Laser colors are chosen to avoid friendly-fire confusion (red vs blue designations)
4. **Personal Preference** - Veterans like Fletch customize extensively; newer recruits use standard configurations
