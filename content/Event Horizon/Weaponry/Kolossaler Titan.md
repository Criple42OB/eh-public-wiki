# Overview
The **Kolossaler Titan** is a massive bipedal mechanical walker deployed by the **Fourth Reich** as a show of overwhelming force. Standing approximately four stories tall, these colossal war machines were developed as enlarged versions of **Mary's titan design** and represent the Fourth Reich's commitment to industrial-scale military superiority. Capable of leveling fortifications and suppressing large concentrations of enemy forces, the Kolossaler Titan serves as both a tactical instrument and a symbol of the Reich's technological advancement.

# History
- **May 2069**: The **Kolossaler Titan Korps** officially established
- **May 2069**: First units rapidly deployed to Norway
- **May 2069**: Decisive deployment during the storm of the Nordic territories
- **June 2069**: Integrated into Fourth Reich military doctrine as primary heavy assault platform

# Military Significance
The Kolossaler Titan's primary value lies in its sheer destructive capacity and psychological impact. Each walker is equipped with heavy rotary cannon systems and missile pods capable of dismantling fortified positions. The integrated plasma torch system provides close-in capability for both structural demolition and anti-personnel support. With their height advantage and firepower concentration, Titans dominate conventional battlefields, making rapid territorial conquest feasible.

# Current Status
The Kolossaler Titan Korps remains a cornerstone of Fourth Reich military strategy, with active deployments across occupied European territories. Their continued production reflects the Reich's prioritization of overwhelming mechanical force as a cornerstone of modern warfare.
