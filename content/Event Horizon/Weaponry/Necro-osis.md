### Necromorph Dispatching Device "Necro-Osis"
**Class:** Promesteel-filtered Electron Dispersal Device
**Faction:** Unaffiliated (Manufactured by Mary)
**Base Specs:** Short-range, Massive necro-injury-inducing.

**Standard Configuration:**
- Electron core filtered by refined Promesteel Prism 
- Quad-Spike design at barrel to enable second firing mode

**Notes:**
- The Necro-Osis was designed as a direct counter to Necromorphs, the electrons filtered through the prism exhibit holy-powers which does significantly more damage to Necromorphs.
- The spikes on the side can extend outwards when the secondary firing mode is enabled, allowing the weapon to shoot out a thin but long beam of electrons. Useful for clearing out small gatherings.
