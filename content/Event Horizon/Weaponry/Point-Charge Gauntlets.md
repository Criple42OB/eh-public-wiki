### Point-Charge Gauntlets
**Class:** Directional Electromagnetic Force Weapon  
**User:** Sydney "Hazard" Navarro  
**Platform:** Modified ODST Battle Armor  
**Base Specs:** Short-range, directional repulsion, emergency anti-structure discharge

**Standard Configuration:**
- A pair of reinforced gauntlets connected directly to the armor's conductive mesh
- High-output point-charge emitters mounted in the palms and forearms
- Vector-control coils for narrowing the discharge into a short beam
- A separate high-capacity battery for operation without draining the armor's primary systems
- HALO targeting and safety control shared with the Repulsion Shield

**Operating Principle:**
- The gauntlets concentrate the armor's point-charge system into a directed force front. They push matter away from the emitter along a selected vector rather than firing a conventional projectile.
- Sydney primarily uses them as a short beam to shove people, weapons, doors, debris, or other obstacles out of the way.
- The output can be widened for crowd control or narrowed for precision work. A reversed field creates a controlled pull for drawing an ally out of danger or retrieving equipment.

**Combat Applications:**
- Breaking melee attacks before contact
- Clearing paths through obstacles or hostile combatants
- Forcing open damaged doors and hatches
- Pushing away debris or pulling an ally from a ledge, blast zone, or collapsing structure
- Combining both gauntlets against a heavy target or structural point

**Emergency Overcharge: Redmatter Discharge**
- The gauntlets can sacrifice their reserve power to produce one destabilized matter discharge comparable to a 100% Dominator shot.
- This is not a simple explosion of energy: the system destabilizes weaponized matter into a dense, unstable projectile mass following the Dominator's principle.
- The discharge leaves the gauntlets as a broad blast rather than a concentrated beam. It can be projected forward, but not thrown effectively; a full Dominator-equivalent shot has an effective mass of roughly 5-10 kilograms before its destabilization effects.
- The isolated emergency battery keeps life-support and movement systems online, but the gauntlets remain offline after firing until serviced.

**Limitations:**
- The short beam loses force as it spreads, and high-output use can heat the gauntlets enough to disable individual emitters.
- Reversed output is less stable than repulsion and requires HALO to maintain a safe pull on a living target.
- The Redmatter Discharge exhausts the separate battery, consumes its prepared matter charge, and risks severe damage to the gauntlets and armor. HALO will restrict the firing sequence if it cannot establish a safe forward vector.

**Notes:**
- The gauntlets are an extension of Sydney's modified armor, not independent firearms. Their field coordinates with the Repulsion Shield to keep Sydney from being thrown backward by her own output.
- Their normal role is force control and rescue support; the Dominator-equivalent mode exists because they have a separate battery, not because ordinary repulsion produces Dominator ammunition.
