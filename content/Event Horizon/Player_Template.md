## Common Patterns to Follow

### Species/Race Combinations
- Pure humans: Human
- Demihumans (animal hybrids): Demihuman (dog girl), Demihuman (catboy), etc.
- Cross-element hybrids: Human-Demon hybrid, Angel-Demon hybrid
- Rare types: Goblin, Angel, Demon, Dragonkin

### Rank Progression Examples
- Officer Track: Colonel, Lieutenant Colonel, Captain, 1st/2nd Lieutenant
- NCO Track: Master Chief, 1st Sergeant, Sergeant, Corporal
- Specialist Ranks: Commander, Admiral, Engineer, Spy

### Essential Background Elements
- **How they joined:** Military recruitment, rescue by Fletch, witnessing tragedy, escaping oppression
- **Key event:** Family tragedy, combat operation, meeting Fletch, personal loss
- **Motivation:** Revenge, protection, duty, redemption, securing family
- **Skills:** Combat specialty (sniper, heavy weapons, mechanic), unique talents

### Equipment Standards
- **Armor:** Always ODST or Mjolnir variants (or equivalent)
- **Colors:** Usually black, dark blue, white, red accents; match squad aesthetic
- **Weapons:** Use established UCG weapons (Exteria line, Anima Works, SRS 99-S5, etc.)
- **Attachments:** Suppressors, laser sights, scopes, grips—show customization

### Special Notes
- Include defining physical features (eye color, scars, hybrid traits)
- Mention equipment quirks or drones carried
- Reference connection to broader world (former home, family status)
