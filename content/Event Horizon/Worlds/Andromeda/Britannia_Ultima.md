# Overview
**Britannia Ultima** stands as the jewel of the British Galactic Empire, serving as the cultural, political, and military heart of an interstellar monarchy spanning countless worlds. Once known simply as Earth's United Kingdom, the planet was renamed upon the establishment of galactic rule and the coronation of Ratlukhashaka the Eternal.

The world itself is a marvel of imperial engineering and natural beauty, verdant rolling hills interspersed with massive Gothic-architecture megacities, orbital stations, and shipyards. The capital city, also named **Britannia Ultima**, sprawls across an entire continent and houses the Imperial Parliament, the Royal Navy Admiralty, and the Crown Palace, a structure so vast it can be seen from orbit.

# History
Britannia Ultima's documented history spans from humanity's emergence on Earth through the present day, with the planet serving as the ancestral cradle of the British Empire itself. The transition from Earth-bound monarchy to galactic sovereignty marked a fundamental transformation in 2024 when the newly crowned Ratlukhashaka, a goblin of mysterious origins who seized control through political maneuvering and military prowess, declared Earth's United Kingdom reborn as a galactic empire.

The establishment of **Crown Expanse**, a network of conquered colonial worlds orbiting Britannia Ultima, cemented the Empire's status as a major galactic power. The attempted colonization of Scrashandif in 1769 marked the beginning of the **Three-Hundred Year War**, a grinding conflict that continues to drain imperial resources while simultaneously forging the Empire's martial legend.

# Modern Era
In 2069, Britannia Ultima remains the nexus of imperial power. The planet hosts the largest imperial fleet yards in known space, orbital academies training officers from a hundred subject worlds, and research facilities probing the nature of both celestial and infernal forces. The monarchy maintains an iron grip on power through tradition, propaganda, and an extraordinarily well-equipped military.

The capital city hosts grand ceremonies, royal courts, and the annual **Convocation of the Crowned**, where nobility from across the empire convene to discuss policy and maintain allegiance to the throne. Public monuments celebrate imperial victories, with statues of the Eternal Monarch adorning every major city center.

# Notes
- Britannia Ultima's shipyards are considered among the finest in known space, capable of constructing heavy carriers in months rather than years.
- The planet maintains strict immigration controls; colonization rights are granted only to those of noble birth or exceptional military service.
- Hidden beneath the capital city lie vast archives containing captured knowledge from both Seraphic and Obsidian sources, the result of centuries of espionage.
- The Royal Navy maintains a standing fleet of over three hundred major warships in permanent orbit above Britannia Ultima.
- Tensions with neighboring Scrashandif remain high; border skirmishes occur regularly despite the nominal ceasefire of recent decades.
