# Overview
The South American Socialist Union formed after a wide spread bombing campaign by the UCG and Silverstone and a book made by Ernesto Cienfuegos Vasquez titled "American Liberation and Collectivization" which outlined the methodology to revolution, reconstruction, and a new version of governmental Marxism. 

The ideology outlined in American Liberation and Collectivization, known as Socialist Democratic-Totalitarianism or otherwise as New Marxism, outlines a state controlled by the people which has total power, in order to achieve egalitarianism and collectivism. The ideology separates itself from traditional Marxism by removing the aspects of the "Dictatorship of the Proletariat" in favor of one party upheld by the people's will, meaning all opposition is removed and all aspects of life are controlled by the people.

# Political Structure 
- **People's Representative:**  Rodrigo Reyes (Figurehead)
- **Shadow Leadership:**  Ernesto Cienfuegos Vasquez (Actual operational control)
- **The Revolutionary Council:**  Military command, labor union leaders, and general high level government officials
- **The People's General Assembly:**  Made up of elected officials from districts, typically union leaders
- **The People's Socialist Party:**  A singular party wherein all citizens must participate, contribute, and uphold

# Key Events 
- **Febuary 2069**: General discontent from mistreatment and neglect strike up riots throughout South America
- **March 2069**: A huge bombing campaign by president Silverstone killing millions fuels the people's anger
- **March 2069**: A book titled American Liberation and Collectivization is distributed throughout South America, lighting the match for revolution
- **April 2069**: Ernesto Cienfuegos Vasquez hyjacks a UCG news network, announcing the liberation of South America
- **April 2069**: Government and economical structures are completely altered, collectivization and rebuilding begin; Brasilia is made into 'Rome' in a day

# Culture & Society
- **Ideology:**  Socialist Democratic-Totalitarianism, AKA New Marxism.
- **Symbolism:**   Red flags, the machete and hammer, the hammer and sickle, El Parajo as a symbol of liberation
- **Propaganda:**   Puts a focus on people participating in the People's Socialist Party, collectivism, and complete equality
- **Education:**  Puts a focus on higher education, socialism, and aiding the revolution.

# Military
The Revolutionary Armed Forces focuses on separating itself from "UCG" dependency, creating new armor, technology, and weapons instead of building it off of UCG technology. All adults are forced in militia defense alongside the RAF, meaning all people are armed to some extent, although not as well equipped as the RAF. All key military figures are UCG defectors.

# Relations
- **UCG:**  Mortal enemy; the SASU seeks to advance into North America to liberate people from capitalism.
- **The Fourth Reich:**  Ideological enemies, SASU outlines a clear disdain for fascism, seeking to destroy it alongside capitalism 
- **The Romanov Restoration:**  Practical alliance, however ideologically opposed
- **The New Maoist Movement:**  Practical and ideological alliance, willing to create a sort of "CominTern"

# Legacy
The South American Socialist Union is a new take on socialism and shows the frustration of Latin Americans as a whole from centuries of exploitation. The SASU seeks to create a new utopian world through all means necessary.
