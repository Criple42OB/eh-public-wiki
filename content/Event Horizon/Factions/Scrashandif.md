# Overview
- Ancient dwarven civilization with unparalleled technological advancement
- Governed by a council of clan leaders from their mountain strongholds
- Followers of the Codex Eternus, an ancient tome recording every slight, betrayal, and debt owed to their people
- Possess technology centuries ahead of most off-world civilizations
- Traditionally neutral in galactic affairs, concerned primarily with their own prosperity and grudge settlements
- Engaged in a blood feud with the British Empire spanning three centuries
- Deep cultural reverence for craftsmanship, honor, and ancestral legacy

## Notable Traits
- Exceptional metalworking and engineering capabilities; weapons and armor of extraordinary quality
- Infamous for unforgiving memory regarding debts and insults, once a grudge is recorded, it is never forgotten
- Preference for mountain fortifications and underground citadels impenetrable to aerial assault
- Master miners and tunnel engineers with settlements spanning entire mountain ranges
- Stoic, serious demeanor with little patience for the machinations of younger civilizations
- Highly isolationist despite their technological prowess, little interest in galactic expansion

## Relations with Others
- **The British Empire**: Ancient blood enemies. The source of numerous entries in the Codex Eternus. War is not sought but will be prosecuted forever if necessary.
- **UCG**: Indifferent. Respect their military discipline but view them as transient.
- **Seraphic Concord and Obsidian Dominion**: Philosophical opposites; limited contact.

## Military
- Known as the **Iron Legions**
- Organized by clan lineages; each clan commands specialized regiments (Mountain Guard, Deep Miners, Runesmiths, etc.)
- Wield weapons forged from rare alloys and inscribed with ancient runes
- Fortified strongholds capable of withstanding siege indefinitely
- Renowned for defensive tactics; rarely initiate aggression but are devastatingly effective when provoked
- Heavy reliance on tunnel networks and underground positions, making traditional orbital bombardment ineffective

## Technology
- Amongst the most advanced in known space, rivaling or exceeding UCG capabilities in select fields
- Specialization in materials science, metallurgy, and structural engineering
- Energy weapons powered by geothermal sources tapped from planetary cores
- Armor and defensive systems of unmatched quality
- Technology is largely kept within Scrashandif borders; trade of advanced weaponry is forbidden by the Codex
