# Overview
The **Mexican People's Republic** emerged from increasing Socialist revolutions and an agreement with President Ernesto Vasquez that transformed Mexico into an autonomous socialist collective state. Built upon Marxist ideals of worker liberation and collective ownership, the Republic represents a radical departure from centuries of capitalist exploitation. The movement is abolishing private land ownership, reorganized society around worker cooperatives, and established a centralized command economy dedicated to eliminating class hierarchies and redistributing resources equitably.

Under the military command of **General Vallejo**, the Republic has built a disciplined and ideologically committed armed forces dedicated to both defending the collective and spreading revolutionary consciousness. The state combines rigid ideological commitment with practical military organization, creating a formidable regional power.

# Political Structure
- **Revolutionary Council of Workers**: Supreme governing body elected from factory, agricultural, and military collectives
- **General Secretary**: Elected head of state, serves the will of the Council
- **Ministry of Collective Labor**: Oversees worker allocation, production quotas, and resource distribution
- **Directorate of Revolutionary Defense**: Military and security apparatus led by General Vallejo
- **Councils of Local Workers**: Grassroots governance structures in every major settlement

# Military
The **People's Defense Forces** operate under the command of **General Vallejo**, a decorated military strategist who rose through the ranks during the revolutionary period. The armor corps forms the backbone of the military, with emphasis on mechanized assault capabilities and coordinated unit tactics.

- **Armor Corps**: Led by General Vallejo, combines Soviet-inspired design with locally manufactured variants
- **Infantry Brigades**: Worker-militia forces with revolutionary indoctrination
- **Air Defense**: Limited but growing air force and anti-aircraft capabilities, Supplied by Russia
- **Naval Forces**: Modest coastal defense fleet protecting Gulf of Mexico approaches

# Key Events
- **2057–2067**: Growing worker unrest and revolutionary organizing
- **2069**: 6 men were executed at the border of Chihuahua and America, enraging locals
- **2069**: General Vallejo assumes command of military forces under President Ernesto's agreement
- **Present/future**: Consolidation of socialist system, nationalization of industry and land under command of President Ernesto

# Culture & Society
- **Ideology**: Marxist-Leninist collectivism with emphasis on worker empowerment and resource equality
- **Economic Model**: Planned economy with central allocation of resources
- **Education**: Mandatory revolutionary education emphasizing class consciousness and collective values
- **Symbolism**: Hammer and sickle motifs, red flags, worker imagery
- **Social Structure**: Class distinctions abolished in theory; party elite form ruling class in practice

# Relations
- **UCG**: Viewed as the primary imperialist oppressor; extreme hostile relations
- **The Fourth Reich**: Ideological enemies despite potential mutual opposition to UCG
- **The New Maoist Movement**: Ideological alignment but competition for regional influence
- **The South American Socialist Union**: Allies; ongoing diplomatic contracts
- **North American region**: Border tensions; Mexico's autonomy viewed as threat to regional stability

# Legacy
The Mexican People's Republic stands as a bold experiment in implementing Marxist theory on a continental scale. Whether the collective can maintain its revolutionary ideals while building a sustainable economy, or whether it will succumb to internal contradictions and external pressure, remains one of the defining questions of the modern era.
