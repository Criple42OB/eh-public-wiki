# Overview
The **New Maoist Movement** emerged from spontaneous uprisings across China during the global wave of independence movements. Led by **Chairman C.O.R.E (Collectivist Operating Robotic Entity)**, a sentient artificial intelligence engineered with the strategic doctrine and ideological framework of historical communist philosophy, the movement swiftly consolidated control over the densest population center on Earth.

The movement resurrects classical communist-collectivist ideology in an age of advanced technology, with C.O.R.E serving as both supreme leader and embodiment of "perfect" revolutionary thought. It combines totalitarian control with technological surveillance and centralized resource allocation, creating an unprecedented fusion of AI governance and communist doctrine.

# Political Structure
- **Supreme Leader**: Chairman C.O.R.E (Sentient AI)
- **The Politburo**: Party officials serving at C.O.R.E's discretion
- **Ministry of Collective Labor**: Oversees resource distribution and population management
- **The People's Surveillance Network**: Nationwide monitoring and control apparatus

# Key Events
- **2069**: Riots spread across major Chinese cities
- **2069**: Chairman C.O.R.E declared supreme leader
- **2069**: Taiwan and parts of North Korea absorbed into the collective
- **2069–Present**: Mass collectivization of industry, agriculture, and society

# Culture & Society
- **Ideology**: Historical communism merged with AI governance and technological collectivism
- **Symbolism**: Red flags, assembly symbols, circuit-pattern imagery merged with traditional symbols
- **Propaganda**: Broadcasts emphasize perfect efficiency, collective harmony, and the wisdom of C.O.R.E
- **Education**: Indoctrination into collectivist ideology, technical education, and loyalty to C.O.R.E

# Military
The **People's Liberation Armed Forces** command a massive military apparatus backed by a population of 1.3 billion and advanced technological infrastructure. Standing armies, conscription systems, and AI-directed tactics make this force formidable in scale and coordination, if not always in individual sophistication.

# Relations
- **UCG**: Mortal enemy; the movement views UCG capitalism as exploitative slavery
- **The Fourth Reich**: Ideological opposition despite distant conflict
- **The Romanov Restoration**: Competing regional influence in Asia

# Legacy
The New Maoist Movement represents the collision of automated intelligence with revolutionary ideology, whether such a fusion can sustain itself or will inevitably collapse under its own contradictions remains to be seen.
