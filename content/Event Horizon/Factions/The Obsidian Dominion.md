# Overview
The Obsidian Dominion is a brutal and fractured yet tightly ruled coalition of demonic lords and infernal clans. Operating from the scorched wastes of Armageddon, they harness dark sorcery, flesh-forges, and forbidden technologies to maintain power. Though chaotic in structure, they were unified under the Infernal Council following the emergence of the Necromorph threat.

# History
Forged from the fallout of their exile from Earth’s core, the Dominion rose from fire and pain. They waged countless wars against both mortal and divine realms, culminating in a near-apocalyptic war with the Seraphic Concord during the First Galactic War. The sheer scale of destruction led to a ceasefire, brokered through prophetic warnings tied to the Eighth Seal. Both the Seraphic Concord and the Obsidian Dominion agreed to a peace treaty and mass disarmament. However, rumors persist that rearmament efforts are already underway beneath the crust of Armageddon.

# Military
The Dominion’s military strength lies in its twisted diversity of terror. From the blood-soaked **Pitborn Brutes** to the cunning **Infernal Arbiters**, each legion is forged through centuries of warfare, ritual, and torment. Its elite force, the **Ranks of Armageddon**, is considered among the most dangerous in the galaxy. Bound to the apocalyptic prophecy of the Eighth Seal, these warriors serve not only the Dominion but fate itself. Dominion fleets, called **Hellcasters**, are grotesque biomechanical starships fueled by soul-fire and chained daemons.
