# Overview
The **Proxima Federation** is a highly autonomous branch of the **United Coalition Government (UCG)** specializing in biomechanical development and high-caliber weapons production. It is also a major distributor of intergalactic loans to banks and private companies. Important documents, including tax records and reports, are routed through the Federation and are sometimes falsified for various reasons.

Rather than operate as a conventional government, the Federation is run by a **Board of Directors**. Board members guide major decisions and oversee powerful sectors of the organization. President **Marcus P. Willow** oversees the board and often has the deciding vote in council-member elections.

The capital, **Sata B Prime**, houses many of the Federation’s factories, biolabs, political buildings, and warehouses. After the Federation bought out the black-hole habitat, it became a refuge for pirates fleeing their enemies and bounty hunters. Their presence has made the habitat increasingly unsafe for ordinary residents. The local government is heavily influenced—and at times bribed—by pirates in exchange for protection.

# History
The Federation was established in **2043** to handle mundane tasks and conduct biomechanical research and development. A major breakthrough came in **2046** with the creation of the **Protogens**: mechanically enhanced biological beings designed to be highly susceptible to influence and control. Many were made for slavery and manual labor, though their versatility also made them popular in combat and industry. Protogens were assigned to a wide range of work, including farming, construction, piloting, and starship repair and maintenance.

As the Federation’s industry expanded, so did its appetite for resources. In **2052**, it introduced the **Planetary Mining Programs**, sending thousands of Federation members—often accompanied by Protogens and other biologically enhanced lifeforms—to extract raw materials from planets of interest. The resources were sold for profit or used to fuel further technological development. The program’s high costs eventually convinced the president that it was unsustainable, prompting proposals to make expeditions more efficient.

In **2054**, the **Extinction Event Program** was approved and given substantial funding. Intended to overcome natural resistance and dangerous wildlife on targeted planets, the program sought to create a Protogen-based super-soldier capable of adapting to harsh environments, excelling in combat, and following orders without question. After numerous failed experiments, the first soldier, **E-001 (Soleil)**, was created in **2055**. The program proved devastatingly effective: its soldiers could destroy life across vast regions within weeks and with minimal resources.

Following E-001, **E-002 (Quasi)** and **E-003 (Phoebe)** were created. In **2066**, Quasi defected after being deployed to clear a small garden planet. The Federation shut down the program, prohibited the production of further Extinction Event soldiers, and nearly halted planetary exploitation. Bounty hunters and expeditionary forces were sent to recover Quasi, but the efforts were unsuccessful.

# Proximust

Proximust is a highly renowned sub-division of the Federation responsible for raw material manufacturing and refining, as well as high caliber weapon production. Armor for The EE soldiers was produced by them along with the **BFFA series** of electron railguns and their own special alloy, **Bluesteel**. Bluesteel was forged specifically for the claws of EE soldiers, having special qualities such as self sharpening, extremely high durability, and the ability to atomically repair itself from minimal amounts of damage.
