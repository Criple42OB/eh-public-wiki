# Overview  
**Nyx** is a breathtaking garden world in the **Zeshen** system, one of the first completely habitable planets discovered by the **TONA** and a rare example of a world that remained pristine enough to become a true colony paradise. Its oceans are a deep emerald green, its forests are dense and vibrant, and its mountain ranges rise in dramatic tiers above the cloudline. A small founding party of just **32 travelers** established the first settlements, creating a world that was intended to be a place of renewal, scientific study, and political separation from the louder crises of the inner systems.

To many, Nyx is the embodiment of what a new age should look like: fertile, peaceful, beautiful, and full of potential. Yet beneath its calm surface lies an important strategic reality, its beauty is one of the reasons the galaxy increasingly looks to it as a center of power.

# History  
Nyx’s early history was defined by ambition and optimism. The TONA’s survey teams identified the planet as a near-perfect candidate for settlement almost immediately, and the first colonists were chosen for their skill, discipline, and ability to endure the difficult early years of establishing a self-sustaining society. Those first decades were marked by slow but steady expansion as the settlers carved out agricultural districts, research outposts, and small coastal capitals in the planet’s most temperate regions.

As the colony matured, Nyx became a haven for political dissidents, scientists, and infrastructure specialists who valued stability over spectacle. The world’s remote and ecologically rich landscapes allowed it to develop without the brutal cycles of collapse seen elsewhere, and it grew into a symbol of what an interstellar civilization could become when it chose soft power over conquest. By the time the galaxy’s major factions began to reorganize around the collapse of old hierarchies, Nyx was already seen as a key prize, valuable not just for its resources, but for what it represented.

# Modern Era  
Today, **Nyx** stands as the political heart of the **Solar Alliance**. The alliance’s capital, **New Geneva**, sprawls across one of the planet’s most stable continental regions and has become a center for diplomatic negotiations, military planning, and elite trade. The world’s forests, lakes, and mountain enclaves are interspersed with massive embassy compounds, private residences, and secure government facilities, giving Nyx the feel of a serene utopia built around the machinery of intergalactic power.

The planet is also home to several iconic locations that have become symbols of the alliance’s identity. **Volare Korsakov**, a lush island of cherry blossom forests and calm lakes, named after the late Nikolai Korsakov, is a favored retreat for high-ranking officials and military leaders. **Gardeyja**, a misty forested region of secluded cabins and private estates, has become a place for peace and retreat. Even the city of **Cascadan**, a more industrialized node on the planet’s surface, reflects the dual identity of Nyx: a beautiful world shaped by immense political ambition.

In orbit, the planet is heavily watched by allied fleets and intelligence assets, and on the surface the luxury of life sits side by side with constant security. Nyx is the sort of place where a mountain retreat can sit next to a military communications array, where a tranquil lake can overlook a global power summit, and where the world’s most dangerous actors quietly do business beneath a sky that looks untouched by war.

# Notes  
- **Nyx** is widely regarded as one of the most valuable and politically important worlds in the **Garden sector**, if not the entire region.
- The **Solar Alliance** uses the planet as both a symbol of unity and a strategic headquarters, making it central to the broader balance of power.
- The planet’s beauty, accessibility, and relative stability make it a favored destination for diplomats, researchers, and wealthy elites alike.
- While many still see Nyx as a peaceful world, its influence has made it a frequent target for surveillance, sabotage, and political intrigue.
