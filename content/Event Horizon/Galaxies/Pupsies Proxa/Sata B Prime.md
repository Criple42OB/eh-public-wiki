# Overview  
**Sata B Prime** is a civlization inhabited by the UCG autonomous **Proxima Federation**. The "planet" is built atop a dyson sphere orbiting the black hole **Citium-Zed36**. The colony is a large hub of import/export goods and trading, however is also ran majority by crime families. In 2055 the governing body was "purchased" by a large pirate entity, leading to the colony becoming a safe-haven for pirates.

The UCG oversight of the area is combatted by the Proxima Federation fabricating tax documents and passport signatures. This leads to a paperless and visa free entry into the system for any criminal/outlaw. 

# History  
The colony was the largest and only exporter of **Protogen** slaves in the early 2050's through mid 2060's, which aided in the buyout by hostile pirates in 2055. 

Despite orbiting a black hole, the large dyson sphere colony manages to fabricate gravity at a large scale. Similar to how starships fabricate their own gravity, but with multiple "hubs" of gravity. There are certain sectors of the sphere that are lacking in gravity, or have stronger gravity. 

Due to orbiting a black hole, the colony lacks greenery and trees. There are only 3 sectors out of the 287 that have any form of trees. One of 3 is a large patch of weed owned by an integalactic mafia, running under the alias "Los Zegas" and lead by a masked individual known only to the public as "Yram".

In 2063, the Proxima Federation began constructing large floating orbital platforms around the perimeter of the dyson sphere. These platforms are still under construction to present day, and are named "Afronia" "Musein" "Hernia" and "Nikona".

In 2069, the colony was attacked by a PMC group known as the "Skittle Squad" leading to mass civil-unrest.
