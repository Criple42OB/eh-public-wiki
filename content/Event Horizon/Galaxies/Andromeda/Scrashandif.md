# Overview
**Scrashandif** stands as a monument to dwarven engineering and resilience. A planet dominated by towering mountain ranges and honeycomb networks of subterranean cities, it represents the pinnacle of underground civilization. The surface, harsh and unforgiving with thin atmosphere and extreme temperature fluctuations, has been largely abandoned in favor of vast underground settlements carved deep into the planet's crust.

The dwarves of Scrashandif have mastered technologies centuries beyond most civilizations, leveraging geothermal energy sources, advanced metallurgy, and runic-inscribed machinery to build an impenetrable fortress society. The capital city of **Karak-Azul**, the Silver Peak Citadel, sits atop the highest mountain peak, accessible only through hidden passages and defended by devastating orbital cannon arrays powered by underground reactors.

# History
The dwarves of Scrashandif have inhabited this world since time immemorial, their lineage stretching back further than most recorded histories. Archaeological evidence suggests the first dwarven settlements emerged during the planet's primordial geological era, with ancestors learning to harness geothermal vents and extract rare mineral deposits from the deep crust.

The emergence of the **Codex Eternus** institutionalized their culture of honor-bound memory and vendetta, transforming individual grudges into civilization-wide philosophy. This ancient tome has guided dwarven policy for over three thousand years.

The **Three-Hundred Year War** with the British Galactic Empire began in 1769 when British Imperial forces attempted to colonize Scrashandif's mineral-rich highlands. The dwarves, unwilling to surrender their ancestral lands, crushed the initial invasion and have since recorded this grievance in perpetuity, every battle, every casualty, every broken treaty inscribed in the Codex Eternus.

# Modern Era
In 2069, Scrashandif remains largely isolationist, focused on the maintenance of their mountain strongholds and the prosecution of their century-spanning vendetta with the British Empire. Trade with other factions is limited and highly regulated, with dwarven arms and advanced metallurgical goods rarely reaching off-world markets due to traditional laws forbidding the sale of their greatest creations.

The **Iron Legions**, organized into seven great clan armies, stand ready for an eternal war they neither seek nor will ever surrender. Surface installations remain militarized, with hidden defense networks capable of turning the entire planet into an impregnable fortress.

# Notes
- Scrashandif's rare metals, including mithril-equivalent alloys, are unmatched in quality but effectively cannot be purchased, the dwarves simply refuse to sell to outsiders.
- The planet's high gravity, 1.3x standard human tolerance, makes colonization by non-dwarven species nearly impossible without protective equipment.
- The oldest section of the Codex Eternus is kept in a vault beneath Karak-Azul, guarded by the most elite of dwarven warriors.
- Scrashandif has two observation satellites of unknown origin discovered in orbit; their purpose remains classified.
