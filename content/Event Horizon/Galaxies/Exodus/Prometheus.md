# Overview  
**Prometheus** stands as the shining jewel of the **Seraphic Concord**, a paradise world bathed in the golden light of its twin suns. With rolling hills of iridescent grass and crystalline lakes that reflect the divine heavens, it is a sanctuary for the angelic population and the focal point of their faith. The atmosphere, rich with calming pheromones and uplifting auras, enhances the serenity that permeates its landscapes.

At the heart of the world lies the **Luminous Citadel**, a radiant city suspended between the heavens and the ground. From here, the **Seraphic Concord** is governed by the divine monarch, Saint Peter, and his celestial council.

# History  
Prometheus has long been the home of the Seraphic race, who believe it was forged by the hands of the celestial gods before their fall from grace. The planet’s creation myth ties into the legend of the **Heavenly Flame**, an artifact of immense power that is said to have ignited the first spark of life on Prometheus.

Throughout history, the planet has served as the spiritual and military center of the Seraphic Concord. During the **First Galactic War**, Prometheus stood at the vanguard against the demonic forces of the **Obsidian Dominion**, its pristine surface scarred by the cosmic battle. The subsequent ceasefire allowed for the formation of the **Eighth Seal Prophecy**, a peace that has yet to be fully realized.

# Culture  
The Seraphic Concord maintains a deeply hierarchical, spiritually focused society. At the top stands **Saint Peter**, the living embodiment of their faith and leader of the heavenly legions. Their cities are built to reflect divine geometry, with grand structures that seem to shimmer with ethereal light. Art, music, and culture here are dedicated to beauty, purity, and the service of the celestial cause.

Angelic citizens are deeply connected to the **Divine Light**, a metaphysical force that grants them power and insight. They live in unity, with little to no social discord. Their education system emphasizes knowledge of the heavens, celestial warfare, and the study of ancient prophecies.

# Military Significance  
Prometheus houses the **Celestial Legions**, elite angelic warriors who defend the Concord and their allies across the galaxy. Their fleets, called **Eclipsers**, sail through the stars, formed of radiant ships that gleam with divine energy. These ships, powered by the very **Heavenly Flame** from which Prometheus was born, can pierce the darkest of cosmic voids.

The **Luminous Citadel** holds strategic importance, not only as a symbol of faith but as a bastion of military leadership, where Saint Peter and his generals devise strategies to protect their people and prepare for the inevitable return of the **Necromorph** and **Obsidian Dominion** forces.
