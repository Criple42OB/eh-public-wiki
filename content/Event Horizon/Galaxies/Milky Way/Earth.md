# Overview  
Once the cradle of human civilization, **Earth** now teeters on the edge of collapse and rebirth. After centuries of war, ecological collapse, celestial invasions, and internal rebellion, the planet has become a scarred testament to humanity’s resilience and hubris. Vast regions lie in ruin, cities turned to ash, forests to deserts, and oceans to acidic wastelands. Yet, in defiance of all odds, life persists.

The emergence of **floating cities** in the 2050s marked a new chapter. Suspended above the irradiated and storm-ravaged lands, these airborne metropolises, like **New Tijuana** and **New Tokyo**, serve as bastions of UCG influence, housing millions in controlled environments powered by dark fusion and gravitational stabilizers.

# History  
Earth’s legacy is stained with cycles of conquest and collapse. The **Fall of God** and the **Birth of Demons** were hidden truths buried by ancient celestial wars, only resurfacing during the **Kola Superdeep Borehole Incident** and the **Venusian Portal Discovery** in the late 20th century. These events signaled humanity’s awakening to forces far beyond their understanding.

The 21st century was dominated by imperialist ambitions, beginning with the United States’ transformation into **The Order of New America (TONA)**. Its brutal expansion triggered global war, nuclear strikes, and the eventual rise of the **Peacekeepers**, leading to the formation of the **United Coalition Against Tyranny (UCT)** in 2019. By 2023, UCT evolved into the **United Coalition of Galaxies (UCG)**, extending Earth’s legacy across the stars, but not without cost.

The **Category 8 Hurricane of 2036** obliterated much of the eastern seaboard, drowning cities and reshaping coastlines. Environmental warfare, glassing runs, and necromorphic infiltration further decimated what remained.

# Modern Era  
In 2069, Earth is a fractured stronghold. The UCG maintains dominant control from its capital in **Washington DC**, fortified by satellite defenses and orbital cannons. However, **Alfifa insurgents** persist in irradiated zones, most notably the **Glassed Barrens of Africa** and the ruins of Central Asia.

Earth also serves as a **symbolic seat of humanity**, despite the relocation of most interstellar political operations to orbital stations and other planets. Pilgrimages to ancient ruins, such as **Old Hampshire**, **Moscow**, or the **Remains of New York**, are common among those seeking to understand the species' apocalyptic past.

# Notes  
- The original **portal to Hell** under the Arctic Circle remains sealed, but its influence lingers.
- The **cryogenic demon vault** launched from Earth is now lost to deep space, its crash-site on **Armageddon** still a classified UCG secret.
- Earth’s gravity and magnetosphere are artificially reinforced after sustaining planetary fractures during the 2030s orbital wars.
