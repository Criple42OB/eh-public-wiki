# Overview  
The **Longsword-class Interceptor** is a large, multipurpose starfighter developed by **Exteria Corporation**. Designed for deep-space combat operations, interception, and heavy bombardment runs, the Longsword is a powerhouse in the UCG starfighter fleet. Despite its starfighter classification, its size and crew complement make it far closer to a small capital ship than a traditional fighter.

Often deployed in squadrons to escort fleets or deliver precision strikes on high-value targets, the Longsword combines formidable offensive capabilities with advanced onboard systems and robust shielding. Its flexibility allows for both short- and long-range combat missions, and its optional payloads, including **Shiva-class Nuclear Missiles** and **Moray Space Mines**, make it especially dangerous in prolonged fleet engagements.

# History  
The **Longsword** was originally conceptualized to fill a critical gap between light interceptors and heavy gunships during the **Orbital Unification Wars**. The UCG required a craft that could deliver devastating payloads while maintaining maneuverability and advanced target-tracking capabilities.

The first Longswords were deployed during the **Battle of Ganymede's Ring**, where they successfully neutralized multiple heavy cruisers through precise nuclear bombing runs. Since then, the design has undergone several iterations to improve its survivability and firepower, resulting in the current Mk. VII models in wide use today.

# Military Significance  
With a range of modular weapons systems and an advanced AI onboard, the **Longsword-class** is often described as the "sledgehammer" of UCG starfighter command. While not as agile as smaller craft, its heavy armor and potent arsenal allow it to dominate engagements where traditional fighters would falter.

The **M9109 50mm MLA cannons** and **ventral-mounted 120mm guns** offer suppressive fire against both starfighters and frigates, while the **ASGM-10 missiles** provide precision anti-capital ship strikes. In missions requiring extreme force projection, a single Longsword equipped with a **Shiva-class Nuclear Missile** can turn the tide of battle with one strike.

# Cultural Significance  
Due to its size, crew, and mission versatility, the **Longsword-class** is often seen more as a symbol of military prestige than a typical starfighter. Elite squadrons known as **Phantom Wings** and **Blackline 8** exclusively fly Longswords, often with customized hull art and ceremonial markings.

They are regularly featured in UCG propaganda showcasing dominance and tactical superiority, and their presence in a battle is often seen as a morale boost to allies and a grim omen to foes.

# Current Status  
Currently, **Longsword-class** interceptors are deployed throughout the UCG's **Border Defense Fleets** and operate in many forward bases, including those on **Callisto**, **Tartarus**, and the **Araxas Forward Belt**. Their role has expanded to include support for elite insertion teams and emergency resupply missions, thanks to their internal payload capacity.

Despite being expensive to manufacture, the Longsword's track record ensures continued investment. Rumors suggest that Exteria is working on a **stealth-variant prototype** for clandestine missions into enemy space.
