# Overview  
The **Prowler-class Dropship** is a stealth-enabled, multi-role aerospace vessel engineered by **Exteria Corporation** for high-risk infiltration, reconnaissance, and rapid deployment missions. Inspired by legacy VTOLs like the V-22 Osprey, it fuses terrestrial mobility with deep-space adaptability, enabling covert operations in the most hostile environments.

Designed for stealth insertions and VIP extraction, the Prowler is the vessel of choice for elite UCG units such as **Task Force Epsilon Star**, known for its silent operation and advanced stealth systems.

# History  
Developed under the **Black Eclipse Initiative**, the Prowler was created to fill the void between atmospheric dropships and stealth frigates. It debuted during the **Scorchpoint Uprising**, proving its capabilities by infiltrating enemy strongholds undetected, thanks to its cloaking systems and baffled drives.

The later integration of a **slipspace drive** expanded its operational range, enabling independent cross-sector deployments without reliance on larger fleet vessels.

# Military Significance  
Capable of carrying **20 passengers** and operated by a **specialized five-member crew**, the Prowler is optimized for missions such as infiltration, extraction, reconnaissance, and VIP escort. While not a frontline combat craft, it can defend itself with **40mm MLA autocannons** and **point-defense lasers** during high-risk insertions or evacuations.

Its **Moray cluster bombs** are instrumental during drop phases, clearing zones or neutralizing threats before troops disembark.

# Cultural Significance  
Nicknamed the **"Ghost Taxi"** by UCG marines, the Prowler commands a near-mythical reputation. Its near-invisible silhouette and silent operation inspire awe, and fear, on and off the battlefield. Crew members often wear their assignment as a badge of honor, symbolizing precision, discretion, and elite skill.

The Prowler features prominently in recruitment materials and propaganda as a symbol of UCG’s surgical might, present only when needed, and gone before it's noticed.

# Current Status  
Actively deployed in **classified operations**, the Prowler remains one of the UCG’s most secretive and valuable assets. Ongoing enhancements include **next-gen composite armor**, **AI-assisted navigation**, and the upcoming **Whisper-class variant**, rumored to feature **quantum cloaking** and **predictive evasion systems**.

With unmatched versatility in both planetary and deep-space theaters, the Prowler is expected to remain in frontline use for decades as the backbone of UCG’s black operations fleet.
