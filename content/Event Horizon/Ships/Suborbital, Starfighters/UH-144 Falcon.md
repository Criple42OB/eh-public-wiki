# Overview  
The **UH-144 Falcon** is a small, agile VTOL aircraft built by **Exteria Corporation**. It is the light utility counterpart to the heavier dropships in UCG service. It flies using tilting ducted fans and can hover, land in tight spaces, and fly low and fast through cities or canyons that a larger craft could not enter.

It carries six troops and is crewed by two pilots and two door gunners on occasion. It is used for squad-level insertion and extraction, close air support, scouting, and medical evacuation. It is not armored for heavy combat, and crews survive by flying low and moving quickly.

# History  
The Falcon was developed to give UCG ground units a flexible aircraft that could operate where Pelicans and Prowlers could not. It first saw action during the **Scorchpoint Uprising** alongside the early **Prowler-class** craft. There the Falcon's short-range maneuverability made it useful for covering the Prowlers during infiltration drops and picking up survivors afterward.

Its role grew during the **Reclamation Campaigns**. Falcons launched from carriers and frigates flew supply and evacuation runs, which later became their most common assignment. Because they are small and cheap, Falcons became widely distributed. They are now found on most carriers and in nearly every theater, which has made them one of the most familiar UCG aircraft.

The Falcon has also spread beyond the UCG. During the collapse of UCG authority, defecting fleets took their Falcons with them, and the private military company **Ravenwing** operates Falcons of its own, some of them captured or stolen. Epsilon, Ravenwing, and Russian forces have all used Falcons against each other in recent operations.

# Military Significance  
The Falcon is the standard "last mile" aircraft for marines. Operators rely on it to move small teams quickly across a battlefield, deliver supplies to isolated positions, and extract wounded personnel from places where larger craft cannot land.

Its **M247H door guns** are its main defense, and crews fire them while the aircraft is moving or hovering. With the optional **M41 rocket pods**, it can provide short-range fire support to troops on the ground, but it is considered a poor substitute for a Sabre or a gunship. It is most often seen supporting larger operations, for example flying escort or reserve beside **Prowlers** and **Pelicans** during an assault.

Falcons have no slipspace drive and no shielding. They need a carrier or frigate to move between systems, and any serious anti-aircraft fire is dangerous to them. Aircrews value its agility more than its armor.

# Cultural Significance  
Marines and ODSTs nickname the Falcon the **"Gnat"** because of its size, and because it tends to stay close to ground troops. Pilots and gunners usually have a close relationship with the squads they carry, and many Falcon crews work with the same unit for years.

In recruitment material and news coverage, the Falcon often appears as a symbol of rescue. Footage of one landing under fire to pick up wounded soldiers is a common image in UCG media. Among Ravenwing personnel and defectors the aircraft carries a different meaning, since many of them once flew Falcons in UCG service.

# Current Status  
The Falcon is **widely deployed** and remains a common sight across the Solar Alliance, the former UCG, and the Ravenwing fleet. Production continues under Exteria, and factions that lack their own manufacturing have taken to buying or stealing them. Planned upgrades include a quieter engine, better armor around the cockpit, and an improved targeting pod.

As the **D77 Pelican** becomes more common, the Falcon is expected to keep its place in light-transport and support roles, since it can operate in places the larger Pelican cannot.
