# Overview

The **UCG Guardian of Angels (GOA)** is a **Heavy Assault Carrier** and flagship of the UCG's specialized deep-space exploration and tactical response force. Originally commissioned as part of **Fleet A-27**, the Guardian of Angels represents a significant advancement in UCG capital ship design, combining the defensive capabilities of a heavy cruiser with the operational flexibility of a fleet carrier. 

The ship is equipped with formidable offensive armament centered around dual MAC cannons, extensive missile batteries for mid-range engagement, and redundant point-defense systems. Its three hangar bays support extended air operations, making it capable of maintaining air superiority over vast areas of operation. The Guardian of Angels serves as both a command center and a self-sufficient combat platform, equipped with the latest UCG technology and systems.

# Experimental Systems

The Guardian of Angels was equipped with the experimental **Lazarus-14A** biological containment and revival system, designed to study and potentially reverse necromorph infection and mutation. All ships in Fleet A-27 carried this cutting-edge experimental technology as part of a classified deep-space research initiative.

# Armament Details

**Primary Battery:**

- Layered defensive grid against fighter and missile threats

**Auxiliary Armament:**
- 12 MK-IX 280mm Gun Batteries
- Hell Jumper Drop Pods for orbital insertion operations

# Flight Operations & Deployment Architecture

Unlike most UCG capital ships that utilize descending platforms or standard hangar bay doors for launch operations, the **Guardian of Angels** features a revolutionary dual-deployment system that significantly enhances rapid response capabilities:

**Dorsal Runway System:**
- Full-length runway complex mounted across the top hull surface
- Allows direct launch of fixed-wing aircraft and atmospheric craft without mechanical platform systems
- Enables continuous launch and recovery operations during combat without compromising hangar accessibility
- Provides redundancy in deployment options for emergency tactical situations

**Lateral Hangar Bays:**
- Three standard hangar bays integrated into the port and starboard hull
- Equipped with conventional hangar doors for rapid bay-door deployment
- Designed for high-throughput sortie generation during sustained operations
- Allows simultaneous runway and hangar operations for maximum operational flexibility

This innovative architecture enables the Guardian of Angels to launch and maintain air operations at rates exceeding comparable capital ships, making it exceptionally effective for fleet-wide air support, rapid-response deployments, and extended air superiority operations. The combination of both systems provides unprecedented operational flexibility and redundancy that has proven invaluable during Admiral Fletch's command of the reformed Fleet A-27.

# History

## Fleet A-27 Deployment

The **Guardian of Angels** was deployed as flagship of **Fleet A-27** on a critical research mission departing from Earth to investigate necromorph activity in the **Dead Zone**. The fleet carried the experimental Lazarus-14A biological systems aboard every vessel, representing the cutting edge of UCG bioweapon and containment research.

## The Breach & Fall

During operations in the Dead Zone, a catastrophic containment breach occurred. The necromorphs, inadvertently unleashed during the research expedition, rapidly overwhelmed the fleet's biological and cybernetic defenses. Within hours, every ship in Fleet A-27, including the Guardian of Angels, fell under necromorph control. The loss of the fleet and its invaluable research represented one of the most devastating incidents in recent UCG history.

## Fleet A-26 Recovery Operation

**Fleet A-26** was immediately dispatched to recover the compromised vessels and neutralize the necromorph threat. In a daring operation that concluded in **April 2069**, Fleet A-26 successfully regained control of all Fleet A-27 ships, including the Guardian of Angels, despite the massive danger posed by the necromorph infestations.

## Current Command

Following the successful recovery operation, **Admiral Fletch** and her specialized tactical squad were appointed to command the Guardian of Angels and lead the reformed fleet. Under her leadership, the Guardian of Angels has been brought back to full operational capacity and serves as the flagship of Fletch's expanded command. The ship now serves as a symbol of the UCG's resilience and determination, with Fletch personally overseeing all operations.
