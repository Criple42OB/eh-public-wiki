# Overview  
The **UCG Sub-Zero** and **UCG Hajen** are the twin behemoths of the **Fleet A-26**, designed for rapid assault, escort missions, and planetary defense operations. Built with the latest UCG military technology, these ships are a symbol of the UCG's growing naval superiority. The vessels are equipped with an array of weaponry and advanced shielding systems, allowing them to operate effectively in both space and atmospheric environments.

The ships' design emphasizes versatility, speed, and firepower, capable of engaging both small strike forces and large capital ships. Their presence in the UCG Navy is essential to maintaining territorial control and enforcing the will of the UCG across contested systems.

# History  
The **UCG Sub-Zero** and **UCG Hajen** were commissioned during the early years of the UCG's formation, created to bolster the military's capabilities in the face of external threats, such as the rising tides of demon incursions and internal resistance movements. Named after the icy and inhospitable planets from which they originated, these ships have been used in numerous key operations during the UCG's expansionist campaigns.

Their combat debut was in the **Battle of Solis Minor**, where both frigates were crucial in defending the UCG's orbital stations from a combined assault by hostile fleets. The twin ships performed admirably under fire, showcasing their toughness and adaptability in high-stakes combat.

# Military Significance  
The **UCG Sub-Zero** and **UCG Hajen** serve as essential components of the UCG's mobile defense forces, particularly in the face of escalating galactic warfare. These ships are often deployed as escort vessels, providing protection for larger capital ships and convoys, or tasked with rapid deployment to neutralize smaller enemy targets.

Their powerful **Mark IV Heavy Coil - 94B1E6/MAC** and missile delivery systems allow them to strike at both long and short ranges, while their **M870 Rampart 50mm Point Defense Network Turrets** ensure their defense against incoming projectiles. Additionally, their **M4093 Hyperion Nuclear Delivery System Silo** gives them the devastating firepower to counter larger threats when necessary.

The inclusion of **Pelican** dropships in their design further enhances their ability to deploy ground forces or assist in planetary defense operations. Their **Shaw-Fujikawa Translight Engine** enables them to travel across vast distances quickly, making them ideal for rapid response missions in volatile regions.

# Cultural Significance  
The UCG Sub-Zero and UCG Hajen are more than just warships; they are symbols of the UCG's military might and its ability to project power across the galaxy. Their presence is a reminder of the UCG's technological superiority and its willingness to engage in full-scale warfare to maintain control.

Both ships have become iconic in UCG naval circles, often seen as the epitome of strategic warfare. Their names are often invoked in battle cries and military speeches, symbolizing the unyielding resolve of the UCG Navy and its commitment to securing the future of the galaxy.

# Current Status  
Currently, the **UCG Sub-Zero** and **UCG Hajen** are stationed near the border of contested systems, where they play a key role in the defense of UCG-controlled space. With the increasing instability in the galaxy and the rising threats from external forces, these ships remain at the forefront of the UCG Navy’s operations, ready to deploy and engage at a moment’s notice.

They are often seen leading task forces into dangerous territories, a testament to their enduring design and the critical role they play in ensuring the UCG’s dominance.
