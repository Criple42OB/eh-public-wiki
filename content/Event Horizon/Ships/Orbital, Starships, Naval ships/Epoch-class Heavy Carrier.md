# Overview  
The **Epoch-class Heavy Carrier** is the flagship of Admiral Niko’s fleet, representing the pinnacle of UCG naval engineering. Designed for both **fleet command** and **heavy fire support**, this colossal ship serves as a mobile command center, capable of coordinating entire fleet operations while delivering devastating firepower. The **Epoch-class** is one of the **Prides of the UCG**, and Admiral Niko personally commands this flagship to project the UCG’s dominance throughout the galaxy.

With its enormous size, the **Epoch-class** is equipped to carry a massive complement of troops, fighters, and vehicles, making it an unstoppable force in both orbital and planetary warfare. Its advanced **Slipspace Drive** and **Quantum Slipstream Drive** allow for swift movement across vast distances, while its formidable armament ensures that it can handle any threat with overwhelming force.

# History  
The **Epoch-class** was developed following the success of earlier UCG carrier designs, incorporating cutting-edge technology and weapons systems. The first ship in this class, the **UCG Conqueror**, was launched as Admiral Niko’s personal flagship during **Operation Confrontation** against the **Obsidian Concord**. Its ability to operate as both a command center and a formidable warship turned the tide in numerous battles.

The **Epoch-class** carriers were initially deployed during the **Battle of Khionis Prime**, where they provided both orbital bombardment and troop transport support. Their presence was pivotal in securing the UCG’s dominance in contested sectors, with the **UCG Conqueror** becoming a symbol of Niko’s strategic genius.

# Military Significance  
The **Epoch-class** is primarily designed for **fleet command** and **heavy bombardment**, capable of coordinating large-scale operations while engaging in direct combat. With a formidable array of **Heavy MAC Cannons** and **Long-Range Artillery**, the ship can devastate enemy fleets and planetary defenses from a distance. The inclusion of **Shiva-class Nuclear Missiles** ensures that the ship can deal with any large targets or powerful enemy warships.

The **Epoch-class** also boasts an impressive defense system, with **Bulwark Point Defense Guns** and **Thunderhead Point Defense Guns** capable of intercepting missiles, fighters, and other threats. The ship’s **Flamethrower Pods** provide close-quarters support during boarding actions or when dealing with enemy forces on the surface of a planet.

The **Epoch-class** is also capable of carrying vast amounts of **Marines**, **ODSTs**, and **heavy vehicles**, including **Main Battle Tanks** and **Force Application Vehicles**, making it a versatile asset in planetary assaults or defense operations.

# Cultural Significance  
The **Epoch-class** is a revered symbol of UCG military power, especially under Admiral Niko’s command. Known for its unparalleled firepower, strategic significance, and the charisma of its captain, the **Epoch-class** has earned a legendary status among the UCG Navy. The ship is frequently seen in military parades, its massive silhouette a reminder of the UCG's might and Niko’s strategic brilliance.

Its reputation for executing flawless combat operations and its role in securing the future of the UCG make the **Epoch-class** a source of inspiration for UCG naval officers and soldiers alike. The flagship is often viewed as the heart of Admiral Niko's fleet, commanding respect from allies and fear from enemies.

# Current Status  
The **Epoch-class Heavy Carrier** remains the flagship of Admiral Niko’s fleet, serving as a cornerstone of UCG military strategy. Constantly deployed to protect UCG territories and engage in pivotal battles, it continues to inspire loyalty within the ranks of the UCG Navy. With the rise of new threats in the galaxy, the **Epoch-class** remains ever-ready to dominate the battlefield and lead the UCG to victory.
