**STRATEGIC OBJECTIVE**

Complete naval pacification and territorial reclamation of the South American continental territory, currently under control of hostile revolutionary forces designated "The Liberation Collective" and associated rogue military units. Objective timeline: 6 weeks.

**FORCE COMPOSITION**

- **Capital Fleet:** 3 Dreadnought-class vessels (*Dominion*, *Perseverance*, *Iron Resolution*)
- **Strike Fleet:** 8 Cruiser-class vessels with orbital bombardment capability
- **Support Elements:** 12 Frigate-class escort vessels, 40 Fighter squadrons, 6 Troop Transport flotillas
- **Ground Forces:** 180,000 Marines deployed across three landing zones

**OPERATIONAL PHASES**

**Phase 1 - Orbital Dominance (Days 1-3)**  
Establish complete air superiority over all major population centers. Destroy identified resistance command structures, communications nodes, and military installations via precision orbital strikes.

**Phase 2 - Population Centers (Days 4-14)**  
Systematic bombardment of resistance-held cities. Destroy infrastructure supporting revolutionary logistics. Civilian casualties are acceptable collateral damage under wartime protocols.

**Phase 3 - Territorial Consolidation (Days 15-42)**  
Marine landing operations in three sectors: North (Venezuela/Colombia), Central (Brazil), South (Argentina/Chile). Ground forces to secure territory and eliminate organized resistance cells.

**Phase 4 - Stabilization (Days 43-60)**  
Installation of martial law. Occupation forces establish provisional government. Arrest and detention of identified leadership figures and support networks.

---

**PRIORITY TARGETS**

- Command Center: Rio de Janeiro administrative sector
- Resistance Leadership: Location of "El Seagull" (Jackal) - PRIORITY CAPTURE
- Military Installations: São Paulo shipyard complex, Brasília defense coordinates
- Communications: Regional broadcast centers, satellite uplinks
- Supply Lines: Agricultural production zones, mining operations

---

**EXPECTED OPPOSITION**

Estimates indicate upwards of 180,000 active resistance combatants. Limited naval capability (primarily captured or salvaged UCG vessels). Ground forces equipped with conventional weapons and some advanced ordnance believed acquired through Alfifa channels.

**THREAT ASSESSMENT:** Low to Moderate. Resistance lacks orbital capability and sustained air defense. Victory probability: 98.921%.
