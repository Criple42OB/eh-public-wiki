**EXECUTIVE SUMMARY**

On 2067-08-19 at 14:47 UTC, the UCGS *Meridian* and its complement of 20,047 personnel were lost with all hands while conducting deep-space dimensional surveys in the ζ-6 Defense sector. No distress signal was successfully transmitted. Recovery of the incident occurred through automated telemetry logs recovered from drift satellites positioned 4.3 AU from incident epicenter.

**INCIDENT TIMELINE**

**14:33 UTC:** *Meridian* reports successful initialization of Dimensional Resonance Scanner (DRS-7) array. All systems nominal.

**14:45 UTC:** Scanner detects anomalous readings. Command log indicates confusion among bridge crew. Last verbal transmission: *"What in God's- contact, we have contact-"* Signal cut.

**14:47-15:32 UTC:** 45 minutes of partial telemetry. Structural integrity reports indicate massive unidentified forces acting upon hull. Describes sensation as "tearing" rather than impact damage. Thermal imaging from satellites shows vessel fragmenting in patterns inconsistent with conventional weapons or collision. Final recorded data shows multiple hull breaches occurring simultaneously across port and starboard sections, defying standard structural failure models.

**15:33 UTC:** Telemetry ceases. Vessel presumed destroyed.
