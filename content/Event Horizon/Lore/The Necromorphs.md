## Summary
The Necromorphs are monstrous beings born from a corrupted divine virus that was intended to cleanse the universe but was instead twisted by demonic influence. Once human, they were transformed into grotesque creatures after being infected with this virus, which reanimates their bodies and distorts their forms into terrifying horrors. As the virus spread through the galaxy, entire worlds were consumed, and their civilizations erased. A majority of galaxies were destroyed, leaving only a few survivors. Their origin and reign of terror can be traced back to Earth, where they were initially imprisoned in the core of the planet before escaping through infernal portals.

## Origin
The Necromorphs were created by a divine weapon known as "Project Lazarus." Intended as a cleansing tool to eliminate corruption and reset creation, the virus was intercepted by demonic forces on Earth and mutated into a far more dangerous form. Banished to the Earth's core by God, they remained dormant until the Soviet Union’s Kola Superdeep Borehole project unearthed evidence of their existence. This discovery led to covert operations across the globe to study the virus, which eventually led to the opening of portals on Venus, allowing the Necromorphs to escape Earth and spread their plague to other worlds.

## Behavior and Physiology
Necromorphs are not merely killers, they are *recyclers* of life. When they kill, they assimilate their victims into a grotesque and horrific new form. This virus reanimates the dead and turns them into a mindless army of mutations. They can reshape the bodies of the deceased, fusing organic matter with metal and other materials to create terrifying biomechanical monstrosities.

The Necromorphs communicate through a collective hivemind, which allows them to coordinate their attacks and spread the virus. Though they can survive in hostile environments such as space or harsh planets, they thrive in organic, populated spaces where they can propagate quickly. Necromorphs are extremely resilient and can adapt to most environments, making them a terrifying threat across the galaxy.

## Influence
- **Earth**: Earth's Necromorph-infected zones are heavily quarantined and constantly monitored by the UCG, who deploy orbital strikes to prevent any further spread. Not many cases of spread are noted, for the most part it is eradicated on earth, and surrounding solar systems and galaxies.
- **Armageddon**: The demon-controlled planet of Armageddon serves as both a prison and a breeding ground for the Necromorphs. The Obsidian Dominion uses them as weapons, either as mindless soldiers or to terrorize enemies.
- **The Eight Seals**: The Necromorphs are directly tied to Seal IV – *The Dead Walk*. This seal marks the resurgence of the Necromorphs as a major galactic force, potentially triggering the final steps in the galactic apocalypse.

## Notable Necromorphs
- **The Harbinger**: A colossal Necromorph that serves as a hive-mind conductor, capable of controlling thousands of smaller Necromorphs. It has the ability to call forth waves of Necromorphs to overwhelm cities and ships.
- **Y'Saleth the Hollowed**: Once an angelic being, Y'Saleth was corrupted and transformed into a terrifying Necromorph by the virus. Now a servant of the Obsidian Dominion, it serves as a warbeast, capable of leading Necromorph armies in battle.
- **The Choir of Flesh**: A group of juvenile Necromorphs that emit an eerie, haunting sound. Their song, which pulses in binary, drives victims to madness and terror before they are assimilated into the hivemind.

## Known Weaknesses
- **Divine Flame**: Weapons imbued with angelic or holy essence are effective at burning away Necromorphs, preventing their regeneration.
- **Pulse Disruptors**: Electromagnetic pulses are known to disrupt the Necromorph hivemind temporarily, causing disarray among their ranks.
- **Isolated Containment**: If cut off from any form of biomass or organic matter, the Necromorphs are slowed significantly in their ability to reproduce and spread.

## Current Status
The Necromorphs continue to exist in the fringes of known space, though they are currently contained under the vigilant watch of UCT forces. However, with the breaking of the seals and the increasing power of the Obsidian Dominion, it is only a matter of time before the Necromorphs rise again in full force. Their ultimate goal is to wipe out all living beings and convert the galaxy into a necrotic wasteland under their control.
