# The Lazarus Virus

## Overview

The Lazarus Virus is a highly infectious pathogen responsible for initiating the Necromorph plague. Its exceptional transmissibility and devastating effects make it one of the most sought-after biological weapons in known space.

## Characteristics

**Infectivity**: The virus possesses an unparalleled ability to infect host organisms with extreme ease, making containment and prevention measures critical to survival.

**Origin of Name**: The designation "Lazarus" refers to the virus's ability to corrupt and reanimate biological matter, creating undead-like creatures.

## Known Strains

The Lazarus Virus exists in **78 known strains**, with approximately **3 sub-strains per known strain**, creating a complex and constantly evolving threat.

### Strain Variations

Different strains exhibit varying:
- Infection rates
- Incubation periods
- Mutation speeds
- Host compatibility
- Symptom manifestations

## Relationship to Necromorphs

The virus is the direct cause of the Necromorph plague, transforming infected organisms into grotesque mutations characterized by:
- Aggressive behavior
- Enhanced physical capabilities
- Pack mentality
- Rapid spread potential

## Threat Level

**Critical:** The combination of the virus's infectivity, the existence of numerous strains, and multiple faction interests makes the Lazarus Virus one of the most dangerous biological threats in known space.
