# Exteria Corporation

## Overview

Exteria Corporation, formerly known as **Palantir**, is the largest arms manufacturer and military research organization in known space. It is responsible for approximately **92.6% of weapon production** across the universe, making it one of the most influential corporations in existence. Its products range from standard infantry weapons to advanced weapon systems and experimental military technology.

Due to its size and influence, Exteria supplies weapons and equipment to governments and military organizations across the galaxy, regardless of their political affiliations or conflicts with one another. Its manufacturing and research capabilities have made it an essential part of the military infrastructure of many nations, meaning that any major disruption to its operations could have consequences across the entire galaxy.

## From Palantir to Exteria

Exteria was once known as Palantir, a large and controversial entity based out of the United States of America (Pre-TONA era). Palantir was a software company specializing in AI surveillance technology, however. After the fascist takeover of America, CEO Peter Thiel had fled to Bolivia and sold off his company to Chinese investors, which they then went forth to transform Palantir into the weapons-producing giant it is today.

## Takeover by Edward Colon

Following an interplanetary operation that resulted in the death of Exteria's CEO, Jackson Rashid, **Edward Colon took control of Exteria Corporation**. This gave Colon control over the largest arms manufacturer in known space, along with its research facilities, production capabilities, and distribution networks.

The takeover has raised questions about what Colon intends to do with the corporation and its resources. Given his previous involvement with the Ironwright records and his own advanced weapons program, there are concerns that Exteria's resources could be used to further his personal projects.

It remains unclear how the takeover happened internally, or whether Exteria's leadership willingly accepted Colon's control. What is known is that one of the most powerful corporations in the galaxy is now under his control, and what he plans to do with it remains to be seen.
