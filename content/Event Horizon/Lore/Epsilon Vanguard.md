# Epsilon Vanguard

> "When the line breaks, Epsilon answers."

The Epsilon Vanguard is a special military organization made up of elite combat units, specialists, and personnel from several different factions. It is designed to handle high-risk operations that conventional military forces are unable to complete, including missions involving hostile worlds, advanced enemy technology, and supernatural threats.

The Vanguard operates across multiple fronts and is often deployed when a situation requires specialized personnel or a level of force that ordinary units cannot provide.

## Origins: Epsilon Star

Before the Epsilon Vanguard existed, there was Epsilon Star.

Epsilon Star was a UCG special operations unit led by Colonel Fletch Engel. It was created to handle some of the most difficult missions the UCG military had to offer, including operations behind enemy lines, the rescue of high-value personnel, and the destruction of enemy ships and installations.

Over time, Epsilon Star became known for its ability to complete missions that other units struggled with. Its operations eventually expanded beyond conventional warfare, with members taking part in engagements against angels, demons, necromorphs, and other threats that were not covered by standard military doctrine.

As the conflicts across the galaxy continued to grow, Epsilon Star became an important part of the wider war effort. Despite the changes that would eventually affect the organization, its original members continued to carry its name and legacy forward.

## The Rebirth: From Task Force to Vanguard

The transition from Epsilon Star to the Epsilon Vanguard occurred as the scale of the ongoing conflicts outgrew what a single special operations squad could handle.

Following Operation Blackbird and the excommunication from the old UCG leadership, Fletch formally dismantled the original Epsilon Star structure and reorganized it into the Epsilon Vanguard.

Rather than operating as a single squad, the Vanguard became a larger command structure capable of coordinating multiple specialized units. The original Epsilon Star members remained at its core, while other squads, reserve personnel, and specialists from allied organizations were incorporated into the organization.

This structure brought Alpha, Bravo, Delta, and the Cardinals under the same command, allowing them to operate as part of a larger force while retaining their individual roles.

With conflicts spreading across Earth, Prometheus, Armageddon, the dead zone, and other locations, the Vanguard was established to coordinate operations across multiple fronts rather than focus on individual missions. Its expanded structure allows it to respond to a wider range of threats without relying on a single squad to handle every situation.

## What the Vanguard Actually Is

The Epsilon Vanguard serves as a coalition-level rapid-response force. Its personnel come from a variety of military organizations and backgrounds, allowing it to handle missions that require capabilities beyond those of a conventional military unit.

Its members include personnel from:

- The former UCG military
- Defected and loyalist fleets
- The Alliance and allied governments
- Special operations branches
- Elite mercenary and intelligence organizations
- Individuals with unique combat or arcane abilities

The Vanguard's primary responsibility is to respond to high-priority threats and carry out operations that require specialized personnel. These include:

- Infiltration and reconnaissance behind enemy lines
- Ship boarding and assault operations
- Anti-angel and anti-demon missions
- Extraction of important personnel and recovery of artifacts
- Sabotage of enemy infrastructure
- Defense of strategically important worlds
- Recovery of lost technology and alien research
- Assaults against heavily fortified enemy positions

Although the Vanguard is primarily a combat organization, its responsibilities extend beyond direct engagements. It can also be tasked with recovering intelligence, securing important technology, and supporting allied forces during major military operations.

## Doctrine: Speed, Shock, and Survival

The Vanguard's doctrine focuses on speed, adaptability, and minimizing unnecessary losses. Rather than engaging in prolonged battles whenever possible, its units aim to reach their objectives quickly and leave before the enemy can effectively respond.

Its operations generally follow four principles:

1. Disrupt enemy forces before they can organize a response.
2. Preserve personnel whenever possible, as losing an operative can compromise an entire mission.
3. Secure the objective, recover necessary intelligence, and extract surviving personnel.
4. Adapt to the situation when standard tactics are ineffective.

This approach is particularly important when dealing with threats such as angels, demons, necromorphs, and advanced war machines. These enemies often require specialized equipment and tactics that conventional forces may not possess.

Vanguard units are expected to adapt to the threats they encounter, even when doing so requires changing the original mission plan. Their ability to operate under these conditions is one of the main reasons they are frequently assigned to high-risk operations.

## Symbolism and Identity

The Epsilon Vanguard's identity is closely tied to its origins as Epsilon Star. Despite the organization's expansion, its original members remain an important part of its history and continue to influence its culture and traditions.

The Vanguard is known for several characteristics:

- Its original members often hold important positions within the wider organization.
- It maintains relationships with allied military organizations but does not always operate in accordance with their expectations.
- Its units are frequently deployed when other forces are unable to complete a mission.
- Many of its members have extensive combat experience and personal histories connected to the conflicts they fight in.

The organization has developed a reputation for surviving difficult operations and continuing to function despite major losses and changes in leadership. Its history, combined with the experience of its personnel, has made it an important part of the wider coalition war effort.

## Why Epsilon Matters

The Epsilon Vanguard plays an important role in the ongoing conflicts across the galaxy. Its ability to coordinate specialized units and respond to threats across multiple fronts makes it a valuable asset to the coalition and its allies.

Its operations range from recovering important intelligence and technology to participating in major battles that conventional forces cannot handle alone. By bringing together personnel with different specialties and backgrounds, the Vanguard can respond to a wider range of threats than its predecessor, Epsilon Star.

The organization's future remains tied to the conflicts it was created to fight. Whether the Vanguard ultimately secures a lasting victory or is forced to continue fighting through increasingly difficult circumstances, it remains one of the coalition's primary responses to threats that exceed conventional military capabilities.

## Current Squads

**Dallas-1** Internally designated: "Alpha":

The original squad, and what the Vanguard evolved from, carrying the legacy of Epsilon Star into the broader command structure. They are the hard-pressed, high-risk response element sent when the mission is impossible and the room is already on fire. Their emblem is a beam of light shooting to the skies, a star resting at the top of the beam. The background is a purplish-blue, all this encompassed in a shield shape. The bottom of the emblem is wrapped with two sabres, and the motto below them: "Cutting through the chaos."

**Stockholm-41 "Cardinals":**

The Cardinals specialize in maintaining discipline and composure during high-pressure operations. They are trained to remain focused in situations where panic or poor decisions could compromise the mission. Their emblem consists of a crimson background crossed by a halo and a knife-shaped cross. Their motto is "Hold the line, hold the soul." The squad is led by Starbound.

**Nassau-6 "Bravo":**

Bravo is the Vanguard's heavy assault squad and is typically accompanied by "Heavy". It specializes in direct engagements, breaching operations, and holding positions under enemy fire. Bravo is often deployed when a position needs to be taken by force or when other units require additional firepower. Its emblem is a black and silver shield divided by a jagged line, representing the damage the squad is expected to withstand. Its motto is "Break the gate. Own the ground."

**Sydney-18 "Delta":**

Delta specializes in precision operations and rapid responses to unexpected threats. It is deployed when a mission requires quick decisions and there is little room for error. Its emblem is a hollow diamond crossed by a single red slash, representing precision and the consequences of a mistake. Its motto is "Solve the problem before it learns your name." Delta helps the Vanguard respond to situations that require a more measured approach than a direct assault.

**Nairobi-81 "Witches":**

The Witches specialize in covert operations and missions in hostile environments. They rely on adaptability and improvisation, particularly when conventional tactics are ineffective or the operation requires a degree of secrecy. Their emblem is a black crescent over a pale moon. Their motto is "We move where the dark is thickest." The Witches are often suited to missions where remaining undetected is just as important as completing the objective.

**Sarajevo-71 "Bosnian":**

Bosnian specializes in urban combat, close-quarters engagements, and operations in heavily damaged or confined environments. The squad is suited to fighting in areas where conventional formations struggle to operate and where maintaining control of a position is particularly difficult. Its emblem depicts a broken fortress rising from a field of ash, representing resilience under siege. Its motto is "The wall falls, the people stand." Bosnian is often deployed to retake positions and secure areas where civilians or allied personnel may still be present.

**Raven-1 "Ravens":**

The Ravens are a specialized unit formed from Ravenwing personnel. They operate beyond the main line of contact, focusing on flanking attacks, tracking targets, and striking enemy forces from unexpected directions. Their role is similar to that of Alpha in some respects, although their operations focus more heavily on working beyond the front line. Their emblem depicts a black bird against a pale, wind-swept sky, with a single eye in the center of its wing. Their motto is "May the wind carry you home."

Alpha remains the original core of the Vanguard, while each of the other squads provides a different set of capabilities. Together, they allow the organization to respond to a wide range of threats and carry out operations that would be difficult for any single squad to handle alone.
