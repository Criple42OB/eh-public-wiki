# Epsilon Vanguard

> "When the line breaks, Epsilon answers."

The Epsilon Vanguard is not a normal military formation. It is a shock force, a spearhead, and a last-resort answer to problems that are too large for conventional units and too strange for standard doctrine. The Vanguard represents the full form of that force: a coalition-level strike command capable of operating across multiple worlds, multiple fronts, and impossible situations.

They are the blade between the world and the apocalypse.

---

## Origins: Epsilon Star

Before they were a Vanguard, they were Epsilon Star.

Epsilon Star began as a high-readiness UCG special operations element under Colonel Fletch Engel, built to handle the missions nobody else could survive. Their work was not restricted to covert raids. They were sent into enemy territory, crashed into dead zones, rescued high-value personnel, and destroyed warships before the enemy could complete their objectives. Their reputation was built from a simple fact: when a mission went wrong, Epsilon was one of two units sent to fix it.

By the time the wars of the galaxy began to spiral beyond normal politics, the original Epsilon Star had become something more than an elite team. It had become a symbol. The squad was famous for handling impossible objectives with terrifying efficiency: boarding hostile craft, extracting trapped personnel, breaching fortified compounds, combating angels, demons, necromorphs, and other forces that had no place in any standard field manual.

In the current timeline, that original identity still matters. Their founders and most senior members are the ones who kept the name alive even after the old chain of command collapsed. The war changed the structure, but the soul of Epsilon stayed intact.

---

## The Rebirth: From Task Force to Vanguard

The decisive moment came when the scale of the conflict exceeded the usefulness of a single elite squad.

During Operation Blackbird and the excommunication from old UCG leadership, Fletch formally dismantled the original Epsilon Star structure and rebranded it as the Epsilon Vanguard. This was not only cosmetic change.

Under the Vanguard model, Epsilon became a broader command layer rather than a single fireteam. The original core members remained central, but they were folded into a larger, more flexible strike network that could absorb special squads, reserve units, and specialists from allied organizations. Alpha, Bravo, Delta, and the Cardinals all became part of the same larger operational identity, with the original Epsilon members acting as its nucleus.

The galaxy had stopped being a place with a single front. It had become a chain of wars, skirmishes, betrayals, and apocalyptic emergencies stretching from Earth and Prometheus to Armageddon, the dead zone, and beyond. A team built to kill one target or breach one site could not survive the war alone. The Vanguard was built to command across crisis zones.

In practical terms, the Epsilon Vanguard is what happens when an elite unit is forced to become an institution without losing its edge.

---

## What the Vanguard Actually Is

The Vanguard is best understood as the coalitions rapid-response elite command.

Its members are drawn from the best of:

- the old UCG military
- defected and loyalist fleets
- the Alliance and allied governments
- special operations branches
- elite mercenary and intelligence personnel
- individuals with unique combat or arcane capabilities that ordinary doctrine cannot explain

They do not simply fight battles. They decide when a battle matters, where it should be won, and how much force is worth spending to win it. Their missions include:

- deep infiltration behind enemy lines
- shipboard boarding operations
- anti-angel and anti-demon strike missions
- extraction of critical personnel and artifacts
- sabotage of war infrastructure
- defense of strategic worlds
- recovery of lost technology or alien research
- breaking through impossible defensive positions

The Vanguard is the force sent when ordinary troops would be slaughtered or when a war has reached the threshold of legend.

---

## Doctrine: Speed, Shock, and Survival

Epsilon's philosophy is simple: do not grind. Do not outlast. Do not stand in place and absorb punishment.

They operate under a doctrine built around four pillars:

1. Breach and disrupt the enemy before they can organize.
2. Preserve the squad. A dead operative is a lost mission.
3. Return with evidence, survivors, and strategic advantage.
4. Adapt the mission to the threat, even if that means breaking doctrine to survive.

That doctrine is why they are so effective against beings and forces that should be beyond conventional military logic. Angels, demons, necromorphs, and ancient war machines all suffer when the Vanguard arrives because the Vanguard never fights like a standard army. They fight like a catastrophe response team with a war machine strapped to their backs.

Their operations are often described as chaotic, brilliant, and wildly overbuilt. Yet they almost always succeed in the narrow ways that matter: access the objective, secure the prize, and leave the enemy reeling.

---

## Symbolism and Identity

Epsilon carries a reputation that is more than tactical. It is remembered as a force of survivors, specialists, and people who personally carry the cost of every impossible mission.

The unit's identity is defined by a few recurring truths:

- their core members are often the ones who become the face of the larger war
- they are not universally admired by institutions, but they are almost always needed
- they are expected to answer when all other units are exhausted
- the people who join them are often the ones carrying old wounds, spiritual burdens, or unresolved histories

This is part of why Epsilon feels both elite and haunted. They are not simply powerful. They are the force that has seen too much and kept moving anyway.

---

## Why Epsilon Matters

The Epsilon Vanguard matters because they are the unit at the center of the war's turning points.

They are the ones sent into places where the rest of the galaxy cannot go. They are the ones who find the impossible technology, survive the impossible battle, and bring back the missing intelligence that keeps the larger war from collapsing. They are the ones who look like a military unit on paper and a legend in practice.

In both the current timeline and the unfinished ideal branch, Epsilon is the blade that cuts open the next chapter.

Whether their future is a clean victory or a broken, desperate survival, one truth remains:

The world will not end in silence. It will end when the line breaks. And by then, Epsilon will already be there.

## Current Squads

**Dallas-1** Internally designated: "Alpha":
The original squad, and what the Vanguard evolved from, carrying the legacy of Epsilon Star into the broader command structure. They are the hard-pressed, high-risk response element sent when the mission is impossible and the room is already on fire. Their emblem is a beam of light shooting to the skies, a star resting at the top of the beam. The background is a purplish-blue, all this encompassed in a shield shape. The bottom of the emblem is wrapped with two sabres, and the motto below them: "Cutting through the chaos."

**Stockholm-41 "Cardinals"**:
The Cardinals are the Vanguard's spiritual and psychological counter-weight: disciplined, unshakable, moulded for combat, and built for pressure. Their symbol is a crimson field crossed by a halo and a knife-shaped cross. The squad exists for the moments when the team must stay composed while the world falls apart. Their motto is: "Hold the line, hold the soul." The squad is led by Starbound.

**Nassau-6 "Bravo"**:
Bravo is the Vanguard's bruiser element, as they are typically accompanied by "Heavy". They are the unit that walks into the mess after everyone else has already made the mistake. They are the ones who take the choke points, hold the breach, and deliver a response so violent it alters the battlefield itself. Their badge is a black and silver shield split by a jagged line, a visual cue for the damage they are expected to absorb. Their motto is: "Break the gate. Own the ground." Every Bravo deployment is built around surviving the first impact and turning that survival into momentum.

**Sydney-18 "Delta"**:
Delta is the Vanguard's precision and response squad: quick, adaptive, and quietly ruthless. They are sent when the enemy has a surprise ready, and the mission cannot afford a second chance. Their insignia is a hollow diamond with a single red slash through it, representing the precision of a clean strike and the cost of a bad decision. Their motto is: "Solve the problem before it learns your name." Delta is the squad that keeps the Vanguard from being trapped by its own momentum.

**Nairobi-81 "Witches"**:
The Witches are the shadow specialists of the Vanguard. They prefer deniable operations, hostile environments, and desperate improvisation, where their instincts and adaptability matter more than standard doctrine. Their emblem is a black crescent over a pale moon, the kind of symbol that looks beautiful from a distance and lethal up close. Their motto is: "We move where the dark is thickest." They are a reminder that not every victory is won by overwhelming force; sometimes the win is simply being unseen until the enemy is already dead.

**Sarajevo-71 "Bosnian"**:
Bosnian is the Vanguard's urban and anti-attrition squad—built for confined battlefields, collapsed infrastructure, and close-quarters chaos. They thrive where the lines are broken and the defenders have to choose between collapse and panic. Their badge is a broken fortress rising from a field of ash, a visual representation of resilience under siege. Their motto is: "The wall falls, the people stand." Bosnian is the squad sent in to reclaim a position nobody else can hold and do it without losing the people they are there to save.

**Raven-1 "Ravens"**:
The Ravens are the Vanguard's second Alpha in essence. Formed from a group of Ravenwing specialists. They operate beyond the first line of contact, shepherding targets, striking from the blind side. Their symbol is a black bird set against a pale wind-swept sky, with a single eye placed in the center of the wing. Their motto is: "May the wind carry you home."

**Alpha** remains the core, but each squad is a different answer to the same question: how do you survive the impossible and still bring the fight home?
