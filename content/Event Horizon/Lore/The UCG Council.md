## Summary
The Council of Five is the hidden and influential ruling body behind the United Coalition of Galaxies. Comprised of five individuals, their identities are shrouded in secrecy, with each member contributing to the shadowy governance of the UCG. Though their exact roles and motives are cloaked in mystery, they wield immense power over the UCG's military, political, and economic affairs. They were the ones who manipulated global events, steering the UCG toward its rise and its eventual domination. The Council operates from the shadows, orchestrating the fate of entire nations while remaining largely unknown to the public.

## Members:

### 01: The Son of the Fallen
- **Position**: UCG Council Member
- **Description**: The eldest son of a prominent UCG bloodline, 01 ascended to the council after his father’s mysterious death from an unknown disease. As a member of one of the most powerful families in the UCG, his role is vital in maintaining the bloodline's influence. He wears basic robes, and his cone-shaped hat conceals his face, adding to his air of mystery. His presence on the council signifies the enduring power of legacy and tradition within the UCG's elite.
- **Notable Traits**: Reserved and stoic, he often speaks little, allowing his actions to define his place on the council. His silence is perceived as a measure of authority, and his upbringing gives him a commanding presence.

### 02: The Manipulator
- **Position**: UCG Council Member
- **Description**: A former high-ranking societal figure, 02 entered the Council under questionable circumstances. After a military operation in New Pakistan led her to realize the UCG's inherent flaws, she manipulated her way into a place of power. Her robes are long and hooded, with a mask that conceals her identity. Her tactical mind and ability to navigate complex power structures make her a formidable force within the Council.
- **Notable Traits**: Known for her sharp intellect and persuasive abilities, 02 is a master of political maneuvering. She often uses subterfuge and manipulation to achieve her goals, making her an unpredictable and dangerous member of the Council.

### 03: The Brother of Fate
- **Position**: UCG Council Member
- **Description**: The younger brother of 01, 03 was brought into the Council after the sudden death of their mother from the same unknown disease that killed their father. His short robes and bulbous hat further obscure his face, adding to his mysterious aura. Despite his youthful appearance, 03 is fiercely loyal to the UCG, taking a more direct approach to political matters compared to his elder brother.
- **Notable Traits**: 03 is known for his emotional drive and intensity, unlike his elder sibling. His position on the Council is often the result of heated debates, as he represents the more aggressive side of the UCG’s policies.

### 04: The Silent One
- **Position**: UCG Council Member
- **Description**: A shadowy figure who never speaks, 04 is an enigma. Their ascension to the Council was the result of a random selection by a group of elites after an old member was assassinated. 04 wears all black robes with gold accents and a cone-shaped black and gold hat that covers their face. No one knows their true origins, and their silence is as imposing as their mysterious presence.
- **Notable Traits**: 04 is an embodiment of secrecy and power. Their silence and reserved nature make them both terrifying and revered. Many speculate that 04 holds knowledge far beyond what is shared with the rest of the Council, and their influence is quietly feared.

### 05: The Eternal Captain
- **Position**: UCG Council Member
- **Description**: The oldest living member of the Council, 05’s origins are shrouded in complete mystery. No records exist that explain how he gained his seat, and he is notoriously tight-lipped about his past. His white robes, adorned with gold accents, and his captain-style hat with a golden and red monkey mask make him a distinctive figure. Despite his age, 05 remains one of the most respected and feared members of the Council.
- **Notable Traits**: 05's vast experience and longevity give him a unique perspective on the political landscape. His mask is said to symbolize his connection to ancient UCG military traditions, and his presence commands both respect and curiosity. His secretive nature and authority make him the most enigmatic and powerful figure on the Council.

## Influence and Operations
The Council of Five operates from behind the scenes, manipulating the UCG’s vast resources to maintain control and shape the course of history. While the general population remains largely unaware of their existence, the Council controls all major decisions regarding military actions, technological advancements, and societal developments within the UCG. They are known to engage in covert operations, including assassinations, mind control, and the orchestration of wars, all to further the goals of the UCG.

Their ultimate aim is to solidify the UCG's position as the dominant power in the galaxy, ensuring the continuation of their reign. The Council's influence extends into all aspects of life, from the military to economic policies, making them the unseen hands behind the UCG's actions.

## Current Status
While their identities are largely unknown to the public, the Council of Five’s influence remains unchallenged. The loyalty of their members to the UCG is unquestionable, but their true motives and the full extent of their power are yet to be fully revealed.
