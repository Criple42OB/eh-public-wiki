**Ernesto Cienfuegos Vasquez “The Jackal”: Demi-dog, Captain**

Born to an agricultural labor family in Caracas, Gull enlisted in the UCG military to provide stability and protection for his hometown and extended family. A skilled demihuman soldier, he quickly rose to 1st Sergeant in Fletch's squad, sending most of his military pay back home to support the collective. However, this patriotic devotion would be tested when the UCG's true nature as a tool of corporate exploitation became impossible to ignore.
