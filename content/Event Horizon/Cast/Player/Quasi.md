**Quasi "Extinction Event": Aquatic Protogen, Experimental Specialist**
Genderless (Any Pronouns)

Background: 
Created different to most protogen kind, being augmented with experimental implants which caused basic external control functions to break, leading to almost perfect free will. This led to the Proxima Federation resorting to heavy indoctrination instead of mechanical brain hijacking. Quasi functioned as an expeditional aquatic soldier, primarily sent to scout and erase threats from future places the Federation wanted to use as a colony, specializing in water covered planets or deep sea recon. After an undisclosed amount of time, Quasi was able to break free from the Federation and lives as an interplanetary explorer, living a nomadic life.

Personality:
Highly absurdist nature, or otherwise known as "just doing shit". Cocky at times with a playful personality. Extremely reckless, often doing really stupid things and not thinking of consequences due to a very headstrong nature. Very alert and on edge around everyone and everything, except when around, or in the ocean or lakes. Misses eating bladderfish on planet 4546B.

Armor: 
"Maxor" Proxima Light-Weight Blue Steel Armor Plating (Experimental)

Primary Weapon(s): 
Claws

Secondary Weapon(s):

Special Equipment:
