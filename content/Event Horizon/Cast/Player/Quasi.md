**Quasi "Extinction Event": Aquatic Protogen, Lieutenat - Experimental Specialist**
Genderless (Any Pronouns)

*"stare" - Quasi*

**Background:**
Created for unparalleled destruction and strength, Quasi was created with experimental cybernetic augmentations, different than most protogen-kind as one of 3 elite "Extinction" soldiers (E-001, E-002 (Quasi), E-003) by the Proxima Federation. These soldiers were sent down on planets of interest to completely wipe out all relevant life-forms or threats, making things easier for the Federation to do their activities on said planet. Unlike the rest of the species, Quasi was an anomaly, created for primarily underwater missions, and unintentionally with free will, leading to the Federation resorting to traditional manipulation and indoctrination tactics, rather than direct control. After being sent to a distant garden planet with beautiful flora and fauna, Quasi had an epiphany, betraying the federation and adopting his own symbol on his cheek armor, leaving the planet and travelling the stars as an interstellar nomad, searching for a new purpose beyond bringing the death of innocent creatures.

**Personality:**
Highly absurdist nature, or otherwise known as "just doing shit". Cocky at times with a playful personality. Extremely reckless, often doing really stupid things and not thinking of consequences due to a very headstrong nature. Very alert and on edge around everyone and everything, except when around, or in the ocean or lakes.

**Anatomy:**
Endo-Skeleton made from an immensely dense and durable titanium alloy, capable of withstanding upwards of 200k+ PSI and statically lifting ~70-100 metric tons, and dynamically up to ~30-50 metric tons. Mental implants for inhuman reaction speed and extremely high cognitive processing. Nanite visor capable of withstanding high impact blows and diffracting light or laser attacks, along with being able to repair itself subconsciously while incapacitated. Self sharpening claws made from a Bluesteel alloy capable of piercing through almost all metals and materials, however they can not be dulled or retracted what so ever. Extremely soft, white fur, hydrophobic and heat insulating by nature. External armor plating is highly bulletproof and durable, with central torso chassis holding an extremely hot internal reactor core for power that emits a soft buzzing sound and leads to high internal temperature along with a pleasantly warm external temperature.
