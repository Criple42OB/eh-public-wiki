**Valerie "Pluto" Way: Female Human, 18, Lance Corporal**

Valerie Way was born to two loving parents in Los Angeles, California. Her mother is the daughter of famous "My Chemical Romance" frontman Gerard Way.
Valerie joined the Solar Alliance's Marine Corps after being inspired seeing a portrayal of Heavy and Luna fighting side by side. She graduated her training camp 2 weeks early, leaving the other marines in the dust figuratively. 

She rose the ranks of the Marine Corps rapidly, attaining Lance Corporal in just under 3 months of service. Subsequently she was placed as Second-in-Command for her fireteam. She also received semi-personal leadership training from David "Wolf" Harris apart of an exercise program revolving around new marine fireteams deployed in response to the Great Eurasian War.

Valerie's fire team was taken off deployment in Europe suddenly, and attached directly to Dallas-1 for assistance in a small operation in Bahía, Brasil. 
Upon entering the operational area, the fireteams UH-144 Falcon was struck by multiple RPG's from local insurgents. They plummeted rapidly, eventually crashing on the outskirts of the city. Valerie was the only survivor, after being assisted from Malice and Lex.
