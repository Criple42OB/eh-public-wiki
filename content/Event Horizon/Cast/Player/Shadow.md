**Lex "Shadow" Woode: Demon/Human Hybrid, 24, 18B Major, Epsilon Star Lead**

*“We’ll handle it, we always do.” - Lex*

Lex Woode is an ex UCG major and current team leader for the highly elite Epsilon Star squad. He is a very charismatic, kind and capable leader who is seen fondly by his squadmates. He is driven by his need to protect others and not let what happened to his last squad in the Tijuana incident happen again. 

**Appearance:**

Woode is a man of slightly above average height and build. He has green eyes and medium-length, wavy black hair which he usually has untied. Despite a few minor injuries in his time on the battlefield he doesn't have any notable scars aside from a few small scratches around his torso and back. He runs a clean shave but often he will have some kind of stubble due to constant missions. He could get a full beard if he let it grow, but he probably won't. Outside of his armor he wears a few different outfits, usually some baggy pants, boots, an undershirt and some kind of jacket, he never goes out without a jacket. No bias but he's pretty handsome :yawn:

**Personality:**

Despite not realizing, Lex has become a beacon of hope for those around him. Always keeping a positive mindset even when things look the worst they ever have; he always tries to bring others up. He is charismatic, kind and very extroverted, seemingly capable of quickly making friends with just about anyone. He is a very beloved core member of the epsilon team. Whether for better or worse he’s quite lenient as a leader but does step in and put the foot down when he needs to. When not being professional, he has a bit of a teaseful personality but he never means most of the playful insults he occasionally throws. He does not show it often but he carries survivors guilt over the death of all but one of his old squad and feels he should have died in the Tijuana incident as well. This event would lead him to be a very selfless person who would sacrifice himself if it means the safety of those around him.

**Background:**

Lex was a demon-human hybrid and elite trauma team operator for the floating city of New Tijuana, sent into the undercity to recover a missing high-value individual. The mission turned into a nightmare when a necromorph outbreak erupted, overwhelming his squad and cutting off any chance of support as his teammates were killed one by one. In the end, only Lex and a fellow operator (and future covert ops squadmate) named Sarah (Juggernaut) survived long enough to secure the asset and escape back to the city above. Instead of being honored, they were given a brutal choice: execution to keep the incident buried, or forced service in a covert operations unit that handles threats beyond what anyone else has seen.
