**Grace "Scarlet Cross" Brynn: Demihuman (Cow Girl), Specialist E4 - Combat Medic**

A military medic working in the middle east, Grace began her service as a field medic in conflict zones across the region. Despite facing discrimination as a demihuman, her exceptional medical skills and unwavering dedication to saving lives earned her respect among the ranks. Everything changed when a hospital she was volunteering at was bombed by Alfifa forces—an attack that killed dozens and buried Grace in the rubble. She was pulled out by Shadow and Wolf, an experience that forged her resolve and convinced her to transition to combat medicine. Now recognized as one of the best combat medics the UCG has to offer due to her mysterious "health potion" and field medicine expertise, she serves as the Echo Detachment medic specialist. Her distinctive white armor with red cross became a symbol of hope on the battlefield. First combat deployment was the emergency descent to Brasilia during Operation Silverstone, where she proved herself under fire, saving multiple lives despite intense enemy pressure.

**Armor:**
- ODST Armor (Medic variant), white with a large red cross on the breastplate and red accents, reinforced medical compartments.

**Primary Weapon:**
- Anima Works Carbine (compact version optimized for close-quarters medical support)

**Sidearm:**
- Anima Works Smart Pistol MK5 with red dot sight

**Special Equipment:**
- Combat medical kit with advanced trauma supplies
- Mysterious "health potion" compound (origin unknown, highly effective)
- Standard issue combat knife
- Portable defibrillator unit
- Emergency triage markers and field assessment tools

**Special Notes:**
- Has to milk herself regularly to avoid pain.
- Her health potion demonstrates unusual medicinal properties; exact composition and origin remain classified.
