**Afrodita "Luna" Bahamonde: DemiHuman (Bunny girl), 29, Lance Corporal - Sharpshooter**

Born in a moon colony to a family of engineers and researchers, Luna grew up in the quiet tranquility of the lunar settlements. Her childhood was comfortable and peaceful, but as she matured, that peace felt increasingly suffocating. She craved adventure and excitement but recognized early that traditional scholarly pursuits weren't her calling, and she had no desire to throw herself into full combat roles. The middle path came in the form of precision marksmanship—as a sniper, she could engage danger from distance, using her intellect and patience rather than pure aggression. Her exceptional eyesight, a trait common to bunny demihumans, made her a natural fit. She underwent advanced training and rose quickly through the ranks to Lance Corporal, serving as the sharpshooter specialist of Echo Detachment. First descent into hot combat as part of the emergency deployment to Brasilia during Operation Silverstone, where she and her fireteam proved instrumental in the squad's survival.

**Armor:**
- ODST Battle Armor (Sharpshooter variant), All black with experimental cloaking technology and reactive camouflage plating, giving her a ghillie suit effect when active.

**Primary Weapons:**
- SRS 99-S5 Anti-Materiel Rifle (with suppressor, adjustable 50x scope, extended magazine, thermal optics)
- Exteria Vigilant DMR (with suppressor, adjustable 20x scope, burst-fire capable)

**Sidearm:**
- Anima Works Smart Pistol MK5 (standard load-out)

**Special Equipment:**
- Experimental active camouflage system for ghillie suit
- Ballistic computer interface (integrated into helmet)
- Long-range target designation laser
- Portable ammunition supply for anti-materiel rounds
