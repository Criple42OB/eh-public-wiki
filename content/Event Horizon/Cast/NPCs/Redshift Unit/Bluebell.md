**Joseph "Bluebell" Quinsley: Male Human, 38, Ex-UCG Admiral current Operative**

One of only three known survivors of the Red Sun massacre, Quinsley carries both the scars and secrets of that catastrophic event. A newly recruited Admiral after being a Captain in Red-sun, his survival of one of the UCG's most infamous military massacres has granted him an almost mythological status among those who know of the tragedy. The trauma of that week fundamentally altered his worldview, transforming him from an ambitious captain into a more philosophical observer of military doctrine and strategy. He now operates in advisory capacities, though still tustles in the field.

**Background:**
- Deserted admiral of the UCG Navy
- Third confirmed survivor of the Red Sun massacre (date classified)
- Officially exiled from active duty, and wanted by the UCG for crimes of deseration
- Decorated for valor in combat engagements prior to the massacre

**Primary weapon:**
- A modified Exteria PDW with a foregrip and holographic sight

**Armor:**
- Standard issue Red-Sun ODST armor with Active Camoflauge Mk2
- An undersuit allowing extra maneuverability 

**Equipment:**
- An experimental pair of blue-tinted glasses allowing the user to see in the dark, orient themselves quickly after being flashed, and potentially see through smoke. Thermal capabilities also theoretically possible in correct conditions.

**Special Notes:**
- Rarely discusses the massacre; survivors' testimonies remain partially classified
- The identity and circumstances of his survival have made him a subject of persistent rumors and speculation
- Potentially related to Edmund Cross
