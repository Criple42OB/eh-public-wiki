**Freudig "Hollenregen" Knabe: Demihuman (Dragonkin), 28, Suppressive fire specialist**

Freudig Knabe, known as "Hollenregen" is a dragonkin demihuman and a highly capable suppressive fire specialist under Castle. He works as a weapons smith with Ravenwing experts when not on duty, crafting and modifying weapons for the battlefield. He lost both parents at the age of five due to terrorist attacks in his city of Baghdad and was raised by his sister for most of his life.

Hollenregen used to work as a weapons smuggler out of Iraq. He once spent time with Sydney, supplying them with weapons before Sydney was captured by Alfifa. His obsession with combat and weapon design has made him a fearsome presence in the field. He is largely considered to be the pioneer of utilizing Redmatter in weapons.

Hollenregen is deeply sensitive and emotionally expressive, commonly breaking down after intense combat. He owns a large collection of stuffed animals and despite working under Castle and Cain for multiple years, he felt rather distant from their means of fighting. 

**Appearance:**
A dragonkin demihuman with a broad, powerful frame and a large tail that requires specially modified armor. His armor is aqua blue with dark blue accents and is built around his physical structure and combat loadout.
