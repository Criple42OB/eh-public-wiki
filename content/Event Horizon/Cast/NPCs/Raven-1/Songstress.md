**Olga "Songstress" Collen: Female Human, 39, Master Sergeant - Scout**

Olga Collen was once a musician on Earth before she was kidnapped by space bandits. After escaping, she developed into a highly capable covert operative known as Songstress Collen, using her musical background and natural charisma to manipulate situations to her advantage when needed. She became known for gathering critical intelligence and orchestrating information warfare against hostile factions, with her work contributing to the downfall of the rogue civilization, Copernod.
She worked as a spy-for-hire for a majority of her career, Though she was hired by Cain and decided to stay with his squad indefinitely. 

Songstress is calm, sharp, and highly manipulative when necessary, using deception, psychological pressure, and carefully hidden motives to extract information and destabilize enemies. Her talent as an operative lies in blending artistry, precision, and ruthless efficiency into a single battlefield persona.

**Appearance:**
A human female operative with a polished, elegant demeanor and a striking presence, shaped by both her musical background and her years as an intelligence specialist. Commonly seen with red lipstick, and neat hair.

**Armor:**
- Modified ODST battle armor, removing almost all "unnecessary" plates to reduce weight.
- A jump pack allowing her to leap further than a average human 

**Primary Weapons:**
- Typically uses Guerrilla tactics to secure weapons, though otherwise a standard issue A-19.
