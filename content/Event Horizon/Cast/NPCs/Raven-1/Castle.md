**Eule "Castle" Abendroth: Demihuman (Owl Girl), 39, Colonel - Squad lead**

Eule, known by the callsign "Castle" is a demihuman owlgirl and a colonel serving under Ravenwing. She was once a childhood friend of Fletch, though they drifted apart after she moved to Berlin for college. Her education was cut short by financial struggles. After moving to Brussels with her family, she lived peacefully for a few years before an Al-fifan terrorist attack struck the city, killing her youngest sister and mother. 
Struck with grief she turned to military service. Failing the initial exam to join the UCG's RRT based out of London, she was left alone in the foreign city. 
She was then reached out to by Chronois Vextra as she had spotted the young owl on the side of the road late at night. Offering her shelter she learned of Eule's story and let her in on the PMC being formed by a old friend of Chronois, Cain Woode. 

Over the next few years, She earned prestige within her small squad of fellow ravens. Eventually growing to become the squad lead of Raven-1. And somewhat close to Cain himself.

**Appearance:**
A brunette owlgirl with a scar along her left eye, Her armor is dark blue and optimized for military combat.

**Armor:**
- Dark blue modified ODST Battle Armor, equipped with an expiermental phase core

**Primary weapon:**
- A stolen modified Exteria rifle with a foregrip and large ACOG style scope

**Sidearm:**
- Stolen Anima Works Smart Pistol MK5

**Trivia:**
- Childhood friend of Fletch Engel
