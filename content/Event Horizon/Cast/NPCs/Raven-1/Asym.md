**Chronois "Asym" Vextra: Angel (Lyafian), 138, Sergeant**

Chronois Vextra is a Lyafian angelic operative most often seen with a single wing, a trait that has become a defining mark of her presence in the field. Beneath her shaded visor, she often zones out to assess enemy positions before they even arrive, using her instincts and tactical awareness to preempt threats before combat begins. With even a single wing, she can protect a squad from taking devastating casualties, making her one of the most dependable soldiers in her unit.

The Lyafian's are known to be rather dissidant from typical Seraph ideology, Chronois was no different. Her parents were executed at a young age for the same crime. Since then she pledged to never allow the Seraph's to indoctrinate her.
After an attack on a Seraph village, she protected a small family. Shielding them with her wings, though in the combat her right wing withstood a majority of the damage, requiring her to amputate it in the field.
She fended the attack by grabbing a rifle off of an attacking demon, until Seraph reinforcements arrived.

After the defense, she was imprisoned, charged with sacrilege. She escaped after 16 years, riding the underbelly of a Seraph supply ship to Earth.
She was then rescued by Songstress, who was still a musician at this time. Taken in and kept in shelter at the musicians estate.
After Songstress was presented with the offer to join Cain, she dragged Chronois along with her.

**Appearance:**
A 1-winged Lyafian  with a single visible wing, a pure silver armored silhouette, and a black-tinted visor. Her presence is often marked by intense focus and a reserved, analytical expression.
