**Samewa "Hammerhead" Umikaze: Demihuman (Shark Girl), 31, Sergeant**

Samewa Umikaze, known as "Hammerhead," comes from an upper-middle-class family and graduated college early with a nursing degree. However, she joined the UCG when tensions in the Middle East escalated, seeking a more direct role in military service. Her skills and discipline allowed her to rise rapidly through the ranks, and Fletch eventually took notice of her talent.

However, before Fletch was able to put in the request to transfer Samewa to her squad, news struck that she had become M.I.A after an attack on a UCG military base in the Middle East.
Unbeknownst to the UCG and Fletch, Samewa had actually been rescued during a salvaging operation going on at the same time as the attack. She was brought back to Cain's ship and given medical treatment, and promptly joined the Ravens after seeing just how fast the UCG had discarded her.
