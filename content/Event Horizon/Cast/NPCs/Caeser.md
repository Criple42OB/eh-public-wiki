**König "Caeser" [Last Name Unknown]: Demihuman (Catboy), Sergeant - Drop Fleet Commander**

**Background:**

König lost both of his parents in Alfifa terrorist attacks, their deaths fueling an unrelenting desire for vengeance. He joined the Spartan-IV program after seeing propaganda from Alfifa in New Prague, eager to channel his rage into military service. Rising through the ranks with distinctive competence and aggression, König was promoted to Sergeant and given command of an entire drop fleet of Helljumpers, a position that reflected both his combat capabilities and his commanding presence.

During Operation Reclamation, König was the leader of the last squad of Nardia. His drop fleet was decimated in what became known as the Fall of Nardia. König walked out of the prowler after being ex-filled as the sole survivor, carrying six bloody pairs of dog tags from his fallen squad members. When recovered, he spoke only one sentence: "They gave 'em hell." Since that day, the weight of their sacrifice has carved itself deeper into his psyche, transforming him from an ambitious soldier seeking personal vengeance into something more hardened, a man bound to honor the memory of those under his command.

**Personality:**

König is marked by stoicism forged through loss. He carries the dog tags of his fallen squad as a constant reminder of his responsibility. While he maintains military professionalism, there is a visible undercurrent of controlled rage and survivor's guilt. He is fiercely dedicated to his remaining troops, having learned through tragedy that those under your command are worth any cost.

**Appearance:**

A demihuman with feline features characteristic of catboys, pointed ears, sharp eyes, and a controlled demeanor. His frame is athletic and muscular, honed through rigorous military training and Spartan-IV augmentation.

**Armor:**

**Primary Weapons:**
- Anima Works A-19 Assault Rifle

**Sidearm:**
- Anima Works Smart Pistol MK5

**Special Equipment:**
- Six dog tags from fallen squad members (worn as a memorial)

**Trivia:**

- Wears his fallen squad's dog tags as a constant reminder of his losses and obligations
