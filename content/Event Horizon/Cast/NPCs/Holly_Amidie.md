**Holly "Death Call" Amidie: Demihuman (Crow Girl), Captain - Lead Squadron Commander**

Holly embodies a contradiction wrapped in feathers and black armor. In the mess halls and common areas of the UCG fleet, she's a whirlwind of energy and enthusiasm, always moving, always exploring, always seeking the next experience or conversation. Despite her outgoing nature and infectious personality, she finds herself something of a paradox among her peers, never quite cementing close friendships despite her genuine efforts to connect. Her eating habits are as distinctive as her personality; she can reliably be found in cafeterias subsisting almost entirely on fries, a quirk that has become something of a legend among the 22nd "Talon" Air Assault Group. However, on the battlefield, Holly transforms completely. The cheerful demeanor evaporates, replaced by a calculated, merciless warrior whose instincts in combat are nearly supernatural. This dramatic shift in personality during operations makes her one of the most effective pilots in her squadron. Her piloting skills and tactical acumen earned her rapid promotion to Captain and command of the 22nd's primary combat squadron, a position she has not only held but excelled within. Her all-black Sabre Assault Craft has become a symbol of precision and death among the UCG's fighter wings.

**Military Background:**
- Captain, UCG Air Corps
- Commander of the 22nd "Talon" Air Assault Group's Primary Squadron
- Pilot certification: Sabre-class Starfighter, Longsword-class Bomber, Prowler-class Dropship
- Combat record: 34 confirmed aerial engagements, 0 losses under her direct command

- Anima Works Smart Pistol MK5 (sidearm for emergency ground operations)
