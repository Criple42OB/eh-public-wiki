**Sarah "Juggernaut": Female Human, Soldier - 68W - Captain**

Calm under fire, composed in chaos, and unwavering in her commitment to her squad, Sarah is the emotional anchor of Fletch's squad. While others burn with rage or carry trauma, Juggernaut maintains the steady center that keeps operations focused and personnel alive. Her serious yet joking demeanor and caring nature have made her the squad's informal conscience, someone whom even the most volatile members defer to in critical moments. Modifications to her Mark VI Mjolnir Armor maximize her defensive capabilities, making her an excellent point-of-contact during breaches and assaults. Her presence is reassuring; squadmates know that if Juggernaut has your back, you will survive the mission.

**Background:**

- Earned "Juggernaut" callsign through unstoppable forward momentum in operations
- Recognized for both combat effectiveness and squad morale management

**Trauma Team Incident:**
Before earning the "Juggernaut" callsign, Sarah served as a trauma team operator in New Tijuana's elite response units, soldiers dispatched to extract wounded personnel and manage high-risk hostage situations. It was during one such operation in the undercity that everything changed. The primary objective was straightforward: extract a VIP from hostile territory and return topside. Sarah's role was to serve as the squad's anchor, the person responsible for ensuring accountability of all personnel during extraction. She ran a tight team, six operatives including herself and Lex "Shadow" Woode, a half-demon hybrid known for his ruthlessness and unconventional tactical approach.

The undercity was always dangerous, but the infection they encountered went beyond anything in their briefing. Necromorphs, abominations that defied conventional military response doctrine, swept through their position with horrifying speed and intelligence. Sarah's training and leadership kept the squad organized during the initial ambush, but there was no protocol for this. No evacuation route, no reinforcements, no retreat that didn't mean death. She watched her teammates fall systematically, made impossible triage decisions with each passing second, and pushed forward primarily because stopping meant everyone dies. Her calm under that pressure, the way she kept moving, kept fighting, kept pulling injured teammates forward, became her defining moment. Shadow noticed it too; the way she never panicked, never hesitated, never sacrificed tactical awareness for despair. In the end, only she and Shadow made it out with the asset, bloodied and traumatized but alive.

The victory was hollow. Instead of commendation, they were offered a choice by the brass: a quiet execution to bury the incident, or forced conscription into a classified covert operations unit tasked with handling threats like the undercity outbreak that most governments would deny ever happened. Sarah's steadiness during the mission, the composure that had kept her alive in that hell, made her valuable to people who needed soldiers who wouldn't break. She accepted the conscription as the lesser evil, never knowing it would eventually lead her to Fletch's squad, where that same unwavering center would become the foundation that holds everyone together.
