**Jihad "Ruck" Harris: Male Wolf (Canis lupus), 4, K9**
Jihad was a wild wolf, native to the forests of Croatia. Not much is known about the earlier life of Jihad, however he was picked up by animal control enforcers after a incident involving a research drone Jihad had dismantled via force. After said incident, he was taken to the UCG's massive animal "sanctuary" and kept for a year. 
During the rescue mission on said facility by Epsilon, Wolf had saved the wolf and brought him aboard the ship as his own wolf. Eventually, Jihad had gone through training and became the first canine to serve in any ODST corps.
