**"Starbound": Female Human, 20, Corporal - "Cardinal" Squad Lead**

Starbound was born in the suburbs of California, Growing up idolizing the UCG military. Eventually growing specifically fond of the elite taskforce Epsilon Star, her #1 idol being Katherine. 
She was devastated when she learned that the taskforce had been exiled, falling into a slump before learning of their re-immergence and the creation of Rising Star. She was one of the first applicants of the program, quickly rising through the program as a great student to Wolf and being tasked with leading 1 of 4 fireteams capable of being deployed to active combat for further accolades. She personally leads the Cardinal Fireteam, members "Westbound" "Eastbound" "Southbound" and "Northbound".
The Cardinals are one of the most effective fireteams in the program, being the only ones deployed along side Epsilon.
