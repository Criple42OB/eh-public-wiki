**José: Male Human, Leader of the United Coalition of Galaxies (UCG) - Ex-Chancellor**

Despite descending from a long line of born leaders, José inherited only the trappings of leadership and none of the substance. A consummate politician, he is a master of manipulation and deception, possessing an almost supernatural ability to say exactly what any room wants to hear. Wealthy beyond measure, he navigates the political sphere with practiced ease, often coming across as an idiot to casual observers, a perception that works entirely to his advantage. José is not stupid; rather, he is strategically positioned at the apex of UCG power, wielding influence through others while maintaining perfect deniability. His leadership style prioritizes personal gain and political continuity over actual governance, making him both a valuable figurehead and a dangerous adversary.

KIA during East Coast bombings by Al-fifa.

**Background:**
- Born into UCG political dynasty with centuries of accumulated power
- Inherited leadership position rather than earning it
- Distinguished himself through political maneuvering and alliance-building
- Controls vast personal wealth and corporate interests

**Position:**
- Chancellor and ceremonial leader of the UCG
- Commander-in-Chief of UCG military (delegated authority)

**Special Attributes:**
- Master orator and persuasionist
- Genius-level political strategist disguised as simpleton
- Extensive intelligence network
- Vast material resources at disposal

**Tools of Trade:**
- Political capital and favors
- Media manipulation apparatus
- Corporate and military resources
- Plausible deniability protocols

**Notable Tendency:** 
Known to cover up military disasters and civilian casualties for political optics (e.g., Grafing residential explosion)
