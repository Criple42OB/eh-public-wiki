**Reyes "Rook" Johan: Male Human, 26, Commander of the 331st ODST Detachment "Low Morale"**

An ambitious UCG officer, Rook commands the 331st ODST Detachment, a unit raised to fill the role once held by Task Force Epsilon Star. He leads with a measured, practical style, preferring clear plans and disciplined teamwork over theatrics. His father served alongside David "Wolf" Harris, and Rook grew up hearing stories of Wolf's service and the cost of keeping a squad alive. He respects his father's old comrade, but is determined to earn his own reputation rather than trade on that connection.

**Background:**
- Son of a UCG veteran who served with Wolf.
- Commander of the 331st ODST Detachment "Low Morale," the UCG unit that succeeded Epsilon Star.
- Focuses on keeping his detachment coordinated and mission-ready under pressure.

**Armor:**
- UCG-issue ODST combat armor, fitted for his command role.
  - White plating with blue accents and unit markings.
  - Standard communications and tactical display systems.
  - Maintains the armor carefully, treating its visible colors as a point of pride for the 331st.

**Primary Weapons:**
- Exteria assault rifle, configured for Mid-range effectiveness.
- Combat knife for emergencies and utility.

**Sidearm:**
- Exteria J17 Pistol

**Special Equipment:**
