**Atago "Prinz" Belfast: Angel - Demihuman (Foxgirl) hybrid, 28, Seraph scholar - Exiled Admiral**

Born on the outskirts of the Kingdom of Heaven, Atago was deeply interested in the history of her people. She took to ancient texts and books to learn more about them, eventually driving her to join the Heavenly Navy Corps. Climbing the ranks, she took control of the HSS Lamburg, a pride of her families heritage. After dozens of active deployments in the ship, she was tasked with assisting the humans during the Fall of Nardia. Instantly her ship and the sister ships dealt extreme damage to the ODS Belphegor, though when the ship began to turn its heading towards them and smoking... Atago evacuated her ship with her top officers, heading towards the UCG Guardian of Angels. Her and her officers were then promptly taken prisoner, as Fletch and the rest of the UCG civilian population were under the impression Seraph kind weren't allowed to produce starships. 
Along with being a great admiral of the Seraph navy, She also wields a sword with masterful precision. 

**Current Status:**
- Technical prisoner to the Solar Alliance, though allowed to roam free as long as she cooperates.
