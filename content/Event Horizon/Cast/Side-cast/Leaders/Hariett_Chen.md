**President Hariett Chen: Female Human, 32, Chancellor**

Chen joined the UCG Starship Navy at the age of 17, climbing the ranks with determination and pure will. Commanding her own fleet of ships by 22, becoming one of the youngest Fleet Admirals in UCG history. Her operational security was once the highest amongst the UCG following her strict guidelines and policies her staff were required to follow, and the standards they were to uphold to make it in her fleet. 

She was promoted to the rank of Admiral of the UCG Starship Navy at 29, and by 30 was considered one of the most feared in the UCG. 
For the years leading up to her seizure of power, she secured her staffs trust and devotion through pure leadership capabilities.

In June of 2069, President Silverstone was executed, and General Shepherd was assumed KIA. Chen, seeing the power vacuum forming. Immediately declared faction-wide that she was to seize complete power of the UCG by all means. She was met with surprising acceptance as the new leader from her people, and virtually no resistance was met.
In the same day, She announced a complete relinquishing of Earth, allowing the rising revolutionary factions to secure control, under the promise that they would assist her and the UCG to take out the fascist threat, Along with a potential assault on the increasingly-fascistic Seraphic Concord.
