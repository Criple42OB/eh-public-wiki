**Scharli Kirsch: Human, 44, Kaiser-Fuhrer of The Fourth Reich**

A cold, patient, and thoroughly sociopathic ideologue, Scharli Kirsch rose from the shadows of the Reich's internal security apparatus to seize the Fourth Reich from Astrid Kessler in the aftermath of the New Prague disaster. Where Astrid wore her ethnic nationalism as a tool of conquest, Kirsch treats it as scripture. He does not merely believe in purity, hierarchy, and "natural order", he believes the Reich's own people are impure until proven otherwise, and that anything or anyone failing the test is raw material. He feels no loyalty, no guilt, and no attachment, and views soldiers, citizens, allies, and even his own officers as instruments to be spent. Those who have dealt with him describe a quiet, courteous man who never raises his voice, which is exactly what makes him terrifying. He has not yet appeared in person before Epsilon; his rule has only been felt through his regime's actions.

**Rise to Power:**
- Served as Astrid Kessler's Head of Internal Security, running the Gestapo-style Secret Police and keeping dossiers on the officers, Gauleiters, and party elite of the Reich
- Took the Fuhrership in late 2069, shortly before the 14th of November, in the aftermath of the New Prague disaster
- At New Prague, Epsilon reached Astrid's office, she recognized Ernesto "Jackal" Vasquez in front of the squad, and a Titan pulled her out before the tower collapsed and became an Anti-Orbital cannon site; Astrid's civilian militia and soldiers were then overrun by Alliance forces
- Presented the loss of New Prague and Astrid's familiarity with an enemy leader as weakness and betrayal
- Astrid fled to Argentina, where she was taken in as a refugee
- Adopted the title of Kaiser alongside Fuhrer, presenting himself as the culmination of the Reich rather than its successor

**Position:**
- Supreme Fuhrer of the Fourth Reich
- Absolute command of the Reichswehr, the Kolossaler Titan Korps, and the Secret Police
- Final authority over the Gauleiters and the High Command
- Head of the Reich's alliance with the Seraphs

**Regime Under Kirsch:**
- Continues the Great Eurasian War against the Romanovs, with Seraph backing; the Reich's push into Russia was already underway before he took power
- Expanded the Secret Police and gave Gauleiters power to execute "impure elements" without trial
- Continues necromorph research in secret facilities; the one Epsilon found in Finland on 14th of November 2069 held the captured Cardinals
- The Seraph marine at that facility declared Alpha to be in the custody of a "Seraph extension of the Reich", showing how close the Reich-Seraph partnership has become under him
- Held fast against Operation Zero Hour: KMS ships appearing across Solar Alliance systems, missiles built to hit warp, and an Alliance cost of 361 squads deployed and only 89 returned

**Special Attributes:**
- Master of surveillance, blackmail, and political purges
- Patient strategist who plans years ahead and never acts in anger
- Total command over the Titan Korps and Secret Police
- Unshakeable conviction, with no hesitation or doubt to exploit

**Armor:**
- Black and charcoal Kaiser's dress uniform with Reich regalia, worn over concealed light armor
- Commands from behind layers of guards and rarely appears at the front

**Weapons:**
- Ceremonial sidearm (engraved, rarely drawn)
- Personal combat knife kept on his person at all times

**Special Equipment:**
- Direct command link to the Kolossaler Titan Korps
- Private archive of Secret Police dossiers on the Reich's entire leadership
