**Saint Peter: Male Angel, Leader of the Seraphic Concord - Patriarch**

The legendary Saint Peter of biblical renown walks among the living. Older, wearier, but infinitely more skilled in the art of war than his histories suggest. As the leader of the Seraphic Concord (The Holy), Peter commands the celestial forces of heaven with the authority granted by divine covenant. Unlike Gabriel's unflinching righteousness, Peter has learned to balance ideological purity with political necessity, making him far more dangerous to his enemies and far more complicated as an ally. His leadership has guided the Seraphic Concord through eons of conflict, and his strategic acumen rivals that of any mortal military commander. Peter represents the organized divine counterforce to chaos, though whether his methods truly serve heaven's will remains an open question.

**Background:**
- Historical figure: First pope and apostle of Jesus Christ
- Continued existence and leadership through millennia
- Established and leads the Seraphic Concord military organization
- Witnessed and participated in countless theological and literal wars

**Position:**
- Patriarch of the Seraphic Concord
- Supreme commander of all angelic military forces
- Keeper of divine covenant and heavenly law
- Advisor to the highest celestial authorities

**Armor:**
- Ancient holy plate armor with modern reinforcements
- Vestments of high office integrated throughout
- Insignia of his position visible on all equipment

**Weapons:**
- Holy longsword (name and history unknown)
- Ancient holy relics for blessing operations

**Special Attributes:**
- Millennia of combat experience
- Master strategist and military commander
- Authority over angelic legions
- Direct communication with celestial hierarchy
- Proficiency in both warfare and diplomacy
