**Jasarva "Patriot" Davra: Human, 52, Supreme Commander of ARCA - Revolutionary Commander**

Born into the ranks of the UCG military, Jasarva rose through the naval officer corps with distinction and honor. His early career showed all the hallmarks of a dedicated starship captain, tactical brilliance, unwavering leadership, and loyalty to the institution. However, as he climbed the ranks, he witnessed firsthand the political corruption and authoritarian consolidation of power that plagued UCG leadership. When internal reform proved impossible and peaceful advocacy fell on deaf ears, Jasarva made the fateful decision to break away. In 2057, at age 43, he led a revolt across North Africa and the Mediterranean, establishing the Alfifan Revolutionary Command Authority (ARCA) as a militaristic alternative to what he saw as UCG tyranny. Now at 52, despite his advancing age, Jasarva remains a formidable combatant and masterful strategist. His mere presence commands reverence among his troops, and his revolutionary fervor shows no signs of waning. He despises the current UCG leadership with a burning passion and has made it his personal mission to dismantle what he views as a corrupt and oppressive regime.
