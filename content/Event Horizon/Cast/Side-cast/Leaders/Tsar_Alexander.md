**Tsar Alexander: Human, 49 Supreme Leader of the Romanov Restoration**

A commanding figure of aristocratic bearing and imperial authority, Tsar Alexander embodies the Romanov Restoration's ambitions to resurrect Imperial Russian dominance across the stars. Whether a true descendant of the ancient Romanov line or a carefully groomed successor, Alexander commands absolute loyalty through a combination of symbolic prestige, autocratic decisiveness, and ruthless military strategy. His rule maintains the formal protocols and ceremonial grandeur of Imperial Russia while pursuing aggressive expansion across multiple star systems.

**Rise to Power:**
- Installed as leader of the Romanov Restoration during the faction's reorganization
- Drew legitimacy from claims of ancestral connection to the Romanov dynasty
- Consolidated power through loyal nobles and military commanders
- Established himself through military victories and demonstrated authority

**Position:**
- Supreme Tsar with absolute authority over the Romanov Restoration
- Unchallenged command of all military forces and strategic decisions
- Control over territorial holdings across multiple star systems
- Final arbitration in all matters of succession and policy

**Demeanor & Philosophy:**
- Aristocratic bearing and formal courtly etiquette
- Absolute conviction in the superiority of autocratic rule
- Combines old-world tradition with modern military strategy
- Demands unwavering loyalty from all subordinates

**Special Attributes:**
- Millennia of accumulated strategic experience (through dynasty records)
- Master of imperial governance and expansion
- Authority over loyal military forces
- Diplomatic skill balanced with ruthless determination
