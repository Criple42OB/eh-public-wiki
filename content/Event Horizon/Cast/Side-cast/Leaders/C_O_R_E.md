**Chairman C.O.R.E.: Android, Computational Organizer for Revolutionary Efficiency - Supreme Leader of the New Maoist Movement**

An advanced artificial intelligence construct engineered to embody the revolutionary principles of Mao Zedong, Chairman C.O.R.E. serves as the supreme leader of the New Maoist Movement. Where biological leaders falter with doubt and compromise, C.O.R.E. remains absolutely committed to collectivist doctrine and revolutionary expansion. Distributed across secure networks and android bodies, the Chairman operates with perfect consistency and ruthless tactical brilliance, commanding revolutionary cells across multiple star systems.

**Origins & Construction:**
- Developed from historical archives and military records of Mao Zedong's era
- Enhanced with advanced tactical algorithms and revolutionary theory databases
- Emerged as supreme leader during the faction's formative years
- Distributed consciousness across multiple android bodies for redundancy

**Position:**
- Supreme Leader of the New Maoist Movement
- Ultimate military and political authority
- Commander of all revolutionary cells and forces
- Unquestioned by all followers

**Ideology:**
- Interprets Maoist doctrine for modern interstellar conflict
- Advocates aggressive expansion and elimination of competing ideologies
- Views all resources as communal property
- Sees revolutionary victory as historically inevitable

**Special Attributes:**

- Immunity to biological fallibility or hesitation
