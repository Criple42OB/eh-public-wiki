**Rodrigo "The Liberator" Reyes: Human, People's Representative**

A charismatic former teacher and labor organizer, Rodrigo Reyes became the face of the South American Socialist Union as its People's Representative. Born in São Paulo to union organizers, he was instrumental in rallying the masses during the 2069 uprising with his rousing speeches and ability to connect with the common people.

While he serves as the public figurehead and symbol of the SASU's values, Rodrigo operates as part of the Revolutionary Council and maintains the philosophical vision of the movement. However, the true operational and strategic leadership remains with **Ernesto "Jackal" Cienfuegos Vasquez**, whose military authority and connections with the Revolutionary Armed Forces allow him to exercise real control from behind the scenes.

Rodrigo is genuinely devoted to the socialist cause and the people, making him an effective representative. He coordinates with the People's General Assembly, delivers speeches on behalf of the SASU, and maintains diplomatic relations with allied factions. His effectiveness as a figurehead is enhanced by his authentic belief in the ideology.

**Distinguishing Traits:**
- Eloquent public speaker
- Deep connection to labor movements
- Genuine ideological commitment
- Respected by both civilians and military
- Acts as primary diplomatic representative
