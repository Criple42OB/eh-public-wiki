**Valerius Aegis: Male Angel, 3182, High Guard of the Kingdom of Heaven/Exarch of the Firmament**

Valerius Aegis was fathered from an Archangel, whom taught him combat and the ways of the light since infancy. Valerius joined the Corps of Heaven, climbing slowly through the ranks before eventually being adorned Exarch of the Firmament, a special designation under the Kingdom of Heaven which at its peak had only ~50 members. 
The designation served directly under Saint Peter, God, and the Archangels. Since the gutting of the ranks by Saint Peter, Only around ~20 members remain. Scattered throughout the galaxies and in hiding.
Valerius, however: Was given an exception to losing his rank or face punishment, and anointed High Guard of the Kingdom of Heaven. He took this position, though semi-unwilling and rather disgruntled from such. 

Valerius Aegis was called to arms with the rest of the Exarchs, and were to be led into battle by his father. In this battle, The Exarchs faced immense demonic resistance, Multiple Exarchs fell in combat. 
During the battle, An ancient demon capable of immense powers pushed through the Holy Frontlines, Valerius's father pushed through the fallen angels, Utilizing his Shield of Righteousness he held back the demon, Allowing Valerius and the rest of the Exarchs to flee under his order. The demon eventually overpowered his father, Ripping his head off, and filling his body with demonic energy which lead to his corruption and death.
Valerius Aegis witnessed this from the skies as he flew away.
