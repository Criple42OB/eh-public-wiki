**Nikolai "Niko" Korsakov: Male Human, 3-Star Admiral - Fleet Commander**

Nikolai Korsakov earned his command position through decades of disciplined naval service and unwavering loyalty to the UCG. His fleet, designated A-26, is one of the most formidable forces in the UCG arsenal, commanding approximately 90 vessels and representing the cutting edge of military power. Captain of the UCG's Geber der Angst, a capital ship whose name translates to "Giver of Fear", Niko commands with an aggressive tactical philosophy that has earned him respect and, occasionally, concern among the upper echelons of command. A peculiar phenomenon surrounds his operations: his fleet and Epsilon Star encounter each other with uncanny regularity, a coincidence that seems to defy odds. This strange luck has made him an inadvertent ally to Epsilon Star on multiple occasions. Despite his ferocious demeanor in combat situations, Niko is remarkably personable and warm when off the battlefield, creating an interesting dichotomy between the fierce admiral and the surprisingly affable man beneath the rank. His loyalty to the UCG, however, faces new tensions as political upheaval within the organization threatens to fragment the military hierarchy.

**Fleet Information:**
- Admiral of UCG Fleet A-26 (3rd Capital Fleet)
- Capital Ship: UCG's Geber der Angst (Keyship-class Capital Ship)
- Fleet Composition: ~90 ships including Strident-class Heavy Frigates and Stalwart-class sub-fleets
- Known for aggressive tactical doctrine and high casualty tolerance in pursuit of objectives
- Task Force designation "Red-Sun" led by Malice attached to fleet at all times.

**Armor:**
- Dress uniform with full Admiral regalia (white and gold accents)
- Combat-capable undersuit with integrated communication systems
- Admiral's ceremonial sash bearing command decorations

**Primary Weapons:**
- Precision plasma rifle (customized for bridge operations)
- High-frequency vibro-blade for ceremonial and practical use

**Sidearm:**
- Standard issue plasma pistol

**Special Equipment:**
- Fleet-wide command interface terminal
- Advanced tactical prediction software
- Secure communication suite for inter-fleet coordination
