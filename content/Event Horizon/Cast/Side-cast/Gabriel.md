**Gabriel: Male Angel, Forgotten Archangel - Exiled**

Once the Right Hand of God and an esteemed member of the highest council, Gabriel watched as the foundations of his faith crumbled beneath bureaucratic compromise. A veteran of countless wars spanning millennia, his ruthlessness and uncompromising commitment to justice made him invaluable and dangerous in equal measure. When Gabriel dared to believe that a humble janitor possessed the reincarnated soul of God Himself, the Council deemed him heretical. For his defiance, they burned his wings, ensuring he could never rise up against them, and attempted to erase all record of his existence. But the angels took mercy; they built him a "cathedral" in Armageddon and banished him there for all of eternity. 

**Background:**
- Former Archangel and Right Hand of God
- Veteran of endless theological and literal wars
- Excommunicated for believing in the divinity of the janitor
- Wings damaged as punishment

**Current Status:**
- Released from Obsidian Captivity

**Armor:**
- Holy Plated Armor (worn and deteriorating)
- Long cape worn to conceal damaged armor sections
- Appearance increasingly weathered with each passing year

**Weapons:**
- Twin Holy Blades: "Justice" and "Splendor"
- Sheaths inscribed with complementary phrases: "JUSTICE IS SPLENDOR" and "SPLENDOR IS JUSTICE"
- Holographic wings displaying Hebrew scriptures (legacy of lost true wings)

**Special Attributes:**
- Reverb-affected voice (consequence of exile)
- Profound disdain for machinery and manufactured weaponry
- Deep-seated grudge against demonic entities
- Centuries of combat experience
- Theological knowledge unmatched among living beings
