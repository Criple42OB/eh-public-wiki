**Kusst "Angenehm" Jung, Demihuman (Duckboy), 26, Engineer/programmer under Mary's command**

Raised in Germany in awful living conditions, A broken home, and poor for most of his childhood. Despite all of this, He was a child prodigy and was clearly ahead of most kids. He graduated highschool a year and a half earlier, Offered plenty of scholarships, and eventually attended Humboldt-Universität zu Berlin on full ride.
In college, He met Mary, and quickly became good friends with her. Though it took him months before he felt fully comfortable with talking to her. He was very skiddish in every lecture, very nervous, very shy, almost to the point of yipping when people talked to him.
After graduating, He was employed along with Mary to a Cybersecurity firm. There, Mary and Kusst undertook a large E-Heist while working with the firm, They stayed undetected for a year and a half before getting caught and promptly fired. Mary and Kusst were separated outside of Kusst's apartment in Berlin. Mary was taken to a detention center, and Kusst was taken to a large internment camp on Mars.
Mary had been found by Fletch and taken under her wing as the squads hacker, Kusst was not as lucky. Kusst was moved around dozens of camps and prisons, only growing more and more traumatized as life went on. Eventually losing all hope, He attempted (and succeeded) to take down the camp he was being held in, Freeing every prisoner and establishing temporary mob-rule of the local town. Eventually, He was caught and taken aboard a random UCG ship (The Prophetic) as prisoner, ready to be executed.
He was chained to a wall, and beat for a week and a half before Fletch's squad had infiltrated the ship the day of his execution.
He now works directly under Mary, and has his own little room attached to her lab complete with plenty of posters and plushies to comfort him.
