**Overall ODST trust: 918/1000**

When a PMC threat raises, The publics trust on any and all ODST's will lower. Leading to lower trust rates and acceptances to planets in the alliance.
The threat system is as follows:
- No influence: A PMC which does not have influence or pose any threat.
- Rising influence: A PMC which has begun operation, and is on few radars.
- Low influence: A PMC which has begun more public-facing operations, leading to atleast one nation outlawing their insignia/logos. 
- Moderate influence: A PMC which operations have led to them being outlawed in atleast one major faction.
- Threatening influence: A PMC whose operation is majority public facing, and/or against civilians. Leading to most/every major faction outlawing their activities, and attempts to locate leadership.
- Overwhelming influence: A PMC whom has begun attacking major factions en-masse, leading to direct action being taken against them.
